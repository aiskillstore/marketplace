# Pricing and labor-rate context

Use GSA CALC+ only when pricing context is selected. Record labor category, search term, level, education, experience, geography, contract source, year, result count, and coverage.

CALC+ rates are published ceiling-rate context. They are not proof of paid rates, market wages, proposed rates, total evaluated prices, or price reasonableness. Explain differences in labor mapping, year, escalation, geography, indirect structure, fee, contract terms, and volume.

Label top-N or filtered samples. Preserve the documented CALC+ keyword operation and inspect the returned labor-category aggregation path exposed by the installed schema. Never use an unverified query alias that silently returns the full corpus.
