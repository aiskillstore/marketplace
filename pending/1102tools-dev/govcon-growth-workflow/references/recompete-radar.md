# Recompete radar

Build candidates from relevant awards, IDVs, task orders, modifications, end dates, option information, spending activity, agency forecasts, and public acquisition notices.

For each candidate record incumbent, agency, PIID and parent vehicle when known, requirement, award and potential amounts, start/end dates, recent activity, likely access constraints, public source, confidence, and next validation step.

An end date is a signal, not a guaranteed recompete date. Account for options, bridge actions, extensions, cancellations, migrations, consolidation, insourcing, vehicle sunsets, and missing modification data. Never state a forecast date as certain without an official notice.
