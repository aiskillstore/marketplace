# Opportunity intelligence

## Discovery

Use approved keywords, NAICS, PSC, agency, place of performance, notice type, set-aside, response-date window, and vehicle constraints. Preserve the original query and every controlled broadening step.

## Notice verification

For a candidate, verify the actual notice record and attachments. Record notice ID, title, agency, notice type, posted and updated dates, response deadline and timezone, set-aside, NAICS/PSC, place of performance, contact, amendment history, attachment coverage, and link.

An `active` flag or search hit is not proof the response deadline remains open. Check the date and amendments. State when attachments are missing or inaccessible.

## Bid screen

Separate public fit evidence from internal fit facts. Assess requirement fit, customer fit, vehicle and eligibility, past performance, staffing, clearance/security, teaming, schedule, competitive position, pricing/margin, compliance burden, and strategic value. Do not issue a bid verdict until all internal categories required by `SKILL.md` are present.
