# Teaming due diligence

Screen public fit using exact entity identifiers, registration status, exclusions, certifications, contract vehicles, agencies, relevant prime and subaward experience, geography, clearances or credentials only when publicly verified, and apparent capability complement.

Then identify non-public diligence the company must perform: references, commitment, workshare, exclusivity, OCI, subcontracting limits, cybersecurity, export control, financial capacity, insurance, data rights, IP, disputes, ethics, and agreement terms.

Public data can identify candidates and risks. It does not establish trust, responsibility, legal suitability, willingness, availability, or a final partner selection.
