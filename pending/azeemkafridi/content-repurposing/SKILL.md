# Content Repurposing

Extract the source's durable ideas and create original, platform-native social posts without copying unsupported claims or excessive source text.

## Workflow

1. Identify the source's audience, key points, evidence, links, ownership, and desired outcome.
2. Select a varied package of hooks, summaries, quotes, questions, carousels, short-form video concepts, and CTAs appropriate to the available assets.
3. Adapt each item with `platform-content-adapter`, preserving attribution and the canonical link where relevant.
4. Use `media-preflight` for any derived image or video plan.
5. Prepare the package as BulkPublish drafts, scheduled posts, or a CSV/API execution plan according to the user's request.

Do not fabricate quotations, transcript wording, statistics, or media that was not supplied.
