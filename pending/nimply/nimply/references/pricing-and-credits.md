## What each plan unlocks

**Every plan, including Free:** the Planner and publishing, the design editor
and templates, basic Visor, one Nimply Page, the API and MCP server, live chat
support.

**Paid plans (or an active trial):**

- AI agents
- AI design, Magic Layers and brand-look learning
- Everywhere
- WhatsApp Business inbox
- X accounts
- Stronger AI models; Visor voice and its image and design tools
- Nimply Pages without the badge, with full analytics and tracking pixels
- Analytics history beyond 30 days
- Filling a Brand Kit from a website with AI

**Agency only:** custom domains for Nimply Pages.

**Not offered on any plan:** white-label branding, lifetime deals.

## Free plan or free trial?

They are different things. Offer one, not both, so the user has one decision.

- **Free plan:** $0 forever, no card. Good for trying the Planner, the design
  editor and one Comment-to-DM automation (1 automation, 50 runs a month).
  No agents.
- **7-day free trial of a paid plan:** needs a card, starts with 250 AI
  credits, one trial per person. The plan's full monthly credits arrive with
  the first payment. This is the way to try agents.

Rule of thumb: if the reason they came is agents or AI design, recommend the
trial. If they only want to schedule posts, the free plan.

## AI credits

- **One credit is worth $0.002.** 1,000 credits is $2.
- Everyone pays the same credits for the same action. Bigger plans include
  more credits; they do not get a discount per action.
- The app shows the cost before anything runs, and charges only for what was
  actually used.
- **Plan credits refill every month and do not roll over** (on yearly plans
  too).
- **Extra credits never expire** and are used only after the month's plan
  credits run out:
  - Add-on: 1,000 extra credits every month for $2 a month ($24 a year), on
    paid plans billed on the web.
  - One-time packs: 1,500 for $2.99, 5,000 for $9.99, 20,000 for $39.99, on
    any plan.

Never say "unlimited AI". Scheduling and publishing posts do not use credits
(X is the exception); AI actions do.

## What things cost in credits

Typical figures. The app's own estimate always wins.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| A caption | about 1–3 |
| An AI image | about 42 with a typical model |
| A single AI design | varies with size and detail; held at up to about 350, charged for what it used |
| A 7-slide carousel | held at up to about 970, charged for what it used |
| Filling a Brand Kit from several sources | usually about 20–80 |
| Learning a brand's look | shown before it starts |
| A text agent run (reply, post draft) | a few credits; see the table in `agents.md` |
| An agent run that makes images or video | more; grows with length and number of images |
| AI video, voice-over, dubbing | grows with length |
| Adapting a caption per channel in Everywhere | about 1 per channel |
| Editing or downloading a design by hand | none |

"Held" means the app reserves the maximum estimate while the job runs and
returns what was not used.

## Estimating a monthly credit budget

Do the arithmetic in front of the user and give a range. Use the per-run
ranges in `agents.md`.

**Example — a café with a reply agent and a weekly carousel**

- Customer Support Agent: about 200 messages a month × 5–30 credits
  = 1,000–6,000
- Designer: 4 carousels a month, each held at up to about 970 and usually
  costing less = up to about 3,900
- Captions and small edits: about 100
- **Total: roughly 1,100 at the very lightest, up to about 10,000 a month**

Most of that range is above Creator's 2,000 and all of it fits Growth's
10,000. A cost-conscious café could start on Creator and add credits ($2 per
1,000) once a real month shows what it uses, or make fewer carousels. Say both
options, and suggest checking actual use after the first two weeks.

**Example — a consultant with a LinkedIn Ghostwriter**

- 3 posts a week ≈ 13 runs × 2–12 = about 25–160 credits a month
- Comfortably inside Creator's 2,000.

Things that move the number most: how many messages a reply agent handles,
how many designs or carousels are made, and any video work. Text is cheap;
images and video are not.

If credits run out mid-month, AI actions stop until the refill or a top-up.

## Choosing a plan

Pick the smallest plan that fits the binding limit, then check credits.

| Situation | Plan |
|---|---|
| Only scheduling a few accounts, trying things out | Free |
| A solo creator or one small business, a few accounts, one or two agents with light use | Creator |
| A business with busy DMs, regular AI design, or a small team | Growth |
| An agency or multi-brand business; many accounts or team members; custom page domains | Agency |

The limit that usually decides it:

- **Team members** (3 / 5 / 15) for teams.
- **Social accounts** (6 / 20 / 100) for multi-location businesses.
- **Credits** for anything design- or reply-heavy. Topping up a smaller plan
  is often cheaper than moving up: 8,000 extra credits a month on Creator is
  $16, where Growth costs $40 more. Moving up makes sense when they also need
  the accounts, seats or agent runs.

## Agencies

Each workspace has its own plan and its own credits. An agency running one
workspace per client pays per client workspace, and each client's credit use
is visible separately, which makes it easy to bill back.

A small agency can also run several clients inside one Agency workspace (100
accounts, 15 team members), with one shared team and one shared pool of
credits. Recommend separate workspaces when clients approve their own posts
or when credit use needs to be billed per client.

There is an affiliate program for people who refer customers: 30% of what a
referred customer pays for their first 12 months, through impact.com. Details:
https://nimply.io/affiliates
