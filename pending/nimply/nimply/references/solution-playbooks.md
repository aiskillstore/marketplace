# Solution playbooks by business type

Snapshot: 2026-11-26. These are starting points, not scripts. Adjust to what
the business actually said: which networks they use, how many messages they
get, who approves. Each playbook is deliberately small. Add more only after
the first setup has become a habit.

Nimply has a page for many more business types than are covered here:
`https://nimply.io/industries/<type>` (real-estate, restaurants, consultants,
lawyers, artists, schools, nonprofits, photographers, musicians, churches,
dentists, doctors, ecommerce, salons, car-dealerships, hotels, fitness,
authors, financial-advisors, contractors) and by role at
`https://nimply.io/roles/<role>`.

## How to use a playbook

1. Find the closest type. Mix two if needed (a café that also sells online).
2. Check every channel against the network table in `product-map.md`.
3. Keep the "start with" list. Offer the "add later" list as next steps.
4. Estimate credits with `pricing-and-credits.md` and pick the plan.
5. Write the proposal using the structure in `SKILL.md`.

Reminder that applies to every playbook: when recommending carousels or
thumbnails, the agent is the **Designer** (with its Carousel or Thumbnail
preset).

## Restaurant or café

**Usually short on:** time to answer "are you open?", "can I book?", "do you
have vegan options?" during service.

- **Connect:** Instagram, Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business (where guests
  message), TikTok if they film.
- **Start with:**
  - **Restaurant Concierge** in "it drafts, you send" mode, fed by a Google
    Sheet with the menu and hours and a Business Profile. It takes booking
    and order requests and passes them to staff; it does not confirm
    bookings in a reservation system.
  - A **Nimply Page** as menu and booking link in the bio.
- **Add later:** Designer for a weekly specials carousel; Evergreen Recycler;
  Comment-to-DM ("comment MENU"); Community Moderator if comments get spammy.
- **Stays with a person:** allergy questions, refunds, complaints and anything
  about payment always go to staff. This cannot be switched off.
- **Plan:** Creator to start (one WhatsApp number). Growth when message
  volume or weekly designs push credits past 2,000.

## Salon, clinic or local service

Hair and beauty, trades, cleaners, repair shops, studios.

**Usually short on:** answering price and availability DMs; posting
before-and-afters consistently.

- **Connect:** Instagram, Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business; TikTok for visual
  trades.
- **Start with:**
  - **Customer Support Agent** in suggest mode, with the price list in a
    Google Sheet and hours in the Business Profile.
  - **Designer** with a Brand Kit for before-and-after carousels from their
    own photos.
- **Add later:** Sales & Lead Agent to capture quote requests as leads;
  Evergreen Recycler; a booking Nimply Page; a Comment-to-DM for a seasonal
  offer.
- **Watch out:** Nimply shares a booking link and captures the request. It is
  not a booking calendar. Keep the booking tool they have.
- **Plan:** Creator.

## Online shop

**Usually short on:** turning interest in comments into visits; answering
"is it in stock?", "do you ship to…?".

- **Connect:** Instagram, Facebook Page, TikTok, Pinterest.
- **Start with:**
  - **Comment-to-DM** on launch and product posts ("comment LINK").
  - **Customer Support Agent** in suggest mode with shipping, returns and
    sizing in the Business Profile, and stock or prices in a Google Sheet.
- **Add later:** Sales & Lead Agent; Affiliate Pin Studio or Designer for
  Pinterest pins; Ad Creative Studio for fresh ad images each week; Everywhere
  to copy product Reels to other channels; a store-to-draft flow through
  Zapier or Make when new products are added.
- **Watch out:** Comment-to-DM sends real DMs only on Instagram and Facebook.
  Nimply does not process orders or payments and does not run ads.
- **Plan:** Creator for a small shop; Growth with busy DMs or regular AI
  design.

## Coach or consultant

**Usually short on:** showing up on LinkedIn every week; following up on
people who ask about working together.

- **Connect:** LinkedIn personal profile; Instagram if they use it.
- **Start with:**
  - **LinkedIn Ghostwriter**, three posts a week, written from their notes
    and wins. They approve each one.
  - A **Nimply Page** with the discovery-call link.
- **Add later:** Trend Scout for topic ideas; Content Repurposer to turn each
  LinkedIn post into an Instagram version; Sales & Lead Agent on Instagram
  DMs; Lead Magnet Builder for a free guide that collects emails.
- **Watch out:** LinkedIn personal profiles have no inbox or analytics in
  Nimply, and company Pages are not available yet. Reply agents cannot work
  on LinkedIn.
- **Plan:** Creator, with room to spare on credits.

## Creator or personal brand

**Usually short on:** getting one piece of content onto every platform;
keeping up with comments and brand-deal DMs.

- **Connect:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, plus Threads or X.
- **Start with:**
  - **Everywhere**, if they post video in one app first, so each new video
    reaches the others with approval.
  - **Clip Director**, if they make long videos or podcasts.
  - Otherwise **Content Repurposer**, run whenever a post publishes.
- **Add later:** Designer with the Thumbnail preset; Community Moderator;
  Personal DM Agent for away hours; Trend Scout; a media-kit Nimply Page;
  Affiliate Pin Studio if they earn from recommendations.
- **Watch out:** videos with licensed music posted on Instagram cannot be
  copied by Everywhere. TikTok inbox is comments only, no DMs.
- **Plan:** Creator; Growth if video clipping and thumbnails are weekly.

## Real estate

**Usually short on:** answering "is it still available?" quickly; turning
listings into posts.

- **Connect:** Instagram, Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business; LinkedIn profile.
- **Start with:**
  - **Sales & Lead Agent** in suggest mode, with listings in a Google Sheet.
    Every enquiry becomes a lead with contact details and what they asked
    about.
  - **Designer** for listing carousels in the Brand Kit.
- **Add later:** HubSpot sync, or lead webhooks to another CRM; Evergreen
  Recycler; Comment-to-DM for "comment DETAILS".
- **Watch out:** keep replies in suggest mode. Prices and availability change
  and a wrong answer is costly; the sheet must be kept current.
- **Plan:** Creator for one agent; Growth for a team.

## Agency or freelancer with clients

**Usually short on:** hours. The same tasks repeated across clients, plus
chasing approvals.

- **Set up:** one workspace per client, each with its own accounts, Brand
  Kit, team and credits. Client approvers get approve / reject in Slack or
  Telegram, so they do not need to learn the app.
- **Start with, per client:**
  - The **approval workflow** on everything.
  - One content agent matched to the client (LinkedIn Ghostwriter, Designer,
    or Content Repurposer).
  - The **failure triage** automation with a Slack alert, so a disconnected
    account is caught the same day.
- **Add later:** Community Moderator and Customer Support Agent as paid
  add-on services; bulk scheduling from CSV; the MCP server so the team can
  plan a client's month from Claude or ChatGPT; n8n or the API for reporting
  across workspaces.
- **Watch out:** credentials are per workspace, so cross-client automation
  needs one key per client. There is no white-label app or client portal.
- **Plan:** depends on the model. A plan per client workspace keeps billing
  clean; one Agency workspace suits a few small clients.

## Startup or SaaS founder

**Usually short on:** time to post at all, while building.

- **Connect:** LinkedIn personal profile, X.
- **Start with:**
  - **LinkedIn Ghostwriter** fed weekly notes (what shipped, what was
    learned). Approving takes a few minutes a week.
- **Add later:** Trend Scout; Content Repurposer for X and Threads versions;
  Designer for launch graphics; the MCP server if they live in Claude Code or
  Cursor and want to draft posts from where they work; webhooks or the API to
  post automatically when a release ships (as a draft for approval).
- **Watch out:** publishing to X uses AI credits.
- **Plan:** Creator.

## Nonprofit, school or church

**Usually short on:** people. One or two volunteers or staff doing
everything.

- **Connect:** Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube.
- **Start with:**
  - **Bulk scheduling** of the term's or campaign's announcements in one
    sitting.
  - **Content Repurposer** to turn one report, newsletter or sermon into a
    month of posts.
- **Add later:** Clip Director for recorded talks or services; Community
  Moderator to keep spam out; Customer Support Agent in suggest mode for
  common questions; a Nimply Page for events and donation links.
- **Watch out:** Facebook Groups are not supported, only Pages. Approval
  matters more here: keep a second person approving where children or
  donors are involved.
- **Plan:** Free to start with scheduling; Creator for agents.

## Gym or fitness studio

**Usually short on:** answering membership and timetable questions; regular
content.

- **Connect:** Instagram, Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business, TikTok.
- **Start with:**
  - **Customer Support Agent** in suggest mode with timetable and prices in a
    Google Sheet.
  - **Designer** for weekly class-schedule and member-spotlight carousels.
- **Add later:** Comment-to-DM for a free-trial offer; Sales & Lead Agent;
  Evergreen Recycler; Everywhere for workout Reels.
- **Plan:** Creator; Growth with busy DMs.

## Regulated fields: health, law, finance

Doctors, dentists, lawyers, financial advisors. Nimply is used here for
education content and admin questions, with tighter rails.

- Keep **every post behind approval** by the qualified person.
- Keep reply agents in **suggest mode**, and use "only reply about" to limit
  them to opening hours, location, booking and fees.
- State plainly that the agent must not give medical, legal or financial
  advice in messages, and set the Business Profile and rules accordingly.
- Do not put patient or client records in a sheet the agent reads.
- The business remains responsible for its own professional and advertising
  rules. Nimply's controls help; they are not a compliance certification.

## Building your own playbook

For a business type not listed, answer four questions:

1. **Where do messages arrive?** That decides whether a reply agent is
   possible (see the inbox column in `product-map.md`).
2. **What do they already have that could become content?** Photos, long
   videos, old posts, a blog, a price list. Pick the agent that starts from
   that.
3. **What do customers always ask?** Put the answers in the Business Profile
   or a sheet before hiring a reply agent.
4. **What link should people end up at?** That is the Nimply Page, or the
   Comment-to-DM message.

Then choose one reply-side piece and one content-side piece, and stop.
