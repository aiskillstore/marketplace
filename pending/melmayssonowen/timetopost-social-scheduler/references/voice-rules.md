# Voice matching

- Read recent posts from the intended account before drafting.
- Match its normal sentence length, casing, punctuation, and vocabulary. Ask before inventing facts, claims, pricing, or outcomes.
- Avoid filler openings and hashtag piles. Keep at most one hashtag unless the user requests otherwise.
- Write a different angle and wording for each platform or account. Do not duplicate a post across channels.
- Keep each draft factual and readable. Preserve user-provided wording when they say it is exact.
