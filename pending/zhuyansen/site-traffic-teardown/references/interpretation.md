# Reading the teardown table

## Growth-engine types (from channel mix)

| Signal | Type | What it means | What to copy / watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct ≥ 60% | Brand / habit | Official status, CLI or bookmark habit. Survives traffic drops. | Hard to attack head-on. Look for niches it under-serves. |
| Organic Search ≥ 50% | SEO engine | Volatile: can double or lose 70% in two months. | Pull `keywords`, see which page type ranks, copy the template. |
| Organic Social ≥ 30% | Social burst | Usually the founder's audience or a launch. Decays in 1–2 months unless converted. | Re-check next month (~$0.08). If it holds, study retention (accounts, email, CLI). |
| Referrals ≥ 15% | Distribution | Listed in docs, directories or partner sites. | Check which referrers; get listed in the same places. |
| Mail ≈ 0% | No owned audience | Nothing retains visitors. | Easy win for the target: newsletter or digest. |

## Backlink heuristics

- Links ÷ referring domains > 50 means sitewide footer/sidebar links or link swaps. Raises DR but helps ranking little.
- A young site with high DR, few referring domains and a Semrush authority score near 0 means directory submissions or bought links.
- DR doesn't decide SEO in long-tail markets. A DR 6 site can out-rank DR 30+ with more pages. Always check keyword count against DR.

## Page-type reading (keywords stage)

Count landing URLs by first path segment:
- `/skills/x`, `/tool/x`, `/p/x` (detail pages) means programmatic long tail. It catches searches for the project or brand names it indexes.
- `/best/`, `/top/` (roundups) means curated intent pages: fewer pages, higher-intent words.
- `/vs/`, `/compare/`, `/alternatives/` means it is taking the competitor's brand searches.
- The homepage only means the site ranks for its own brand and has little SEO.

## Opportunity templates

1. **Copy the winning page type.** "X ranks N of its top 20 keywords from detail pages; we have M. Build that template for our items."
2. **Take traffic from a falling SEO site.** "Y fell Z% but still gets S% from search. Its ranking words are up for grabs."
3. **Rank on the rival's brand.** "We already rank #K for 'rival' (volume V). A dedicated vs/alternatives page can reach the top 10."
4. **Convert the burst.** "Social is P% of our traffic, but mail is 0%. Add a digest signup."
5. **Go vertical.** "A niche-only site grew G% on search with DR equal to ours. Split our catalog into vertical hubs."

## Caveats to state

- Similarweb is modelled. Small sites (< 50k/mo) can be off by multiples. If the user has GA or Cloudflare numbers for their own site, compare them; the gap makes a good story.
- Semrush `us` versus worldwide traffic gaps: say which you quote.
- Keyword stage returns the top ~20 rows only.
- Single month of data for brand-new sites: label it "launch month".
