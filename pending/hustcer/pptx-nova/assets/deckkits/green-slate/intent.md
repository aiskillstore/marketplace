# Green Slate

A dark strategy report: charcoal holds the argument, forest green defines structural stages, mint
marks measured value and the next decision. Thin rules and generous outer margins organize dense
evidence. The angled cover/closing field, compact section tab, native comparison matrix and gated
roadmap are identity elements. Exact tokens, frames and capacities live in `design.json`.

Use assertions with a basis, source, as-of and a specific verdict. All claims, measurements, dates,
phase names and labels come from content. The skin does not impose an AI strategy, savings forecast,
investment thesis or organizational policy. Bundled examples use explicitly synthetic scenarios.

Prefer native charts and tables over drawn replicas. The interval chart stacks a lower bound and
the remaining span, with exact low/high strings below; it is not a floating-range plot. Native line
series retain raw values, including negative and tiny observations. A shared linear axis cannot make
every tiny value separately visible. Units, captions and interpretation must explain what is shown.
The supporting allocation chart is a separate native plot and may have its own measure/unit.

Choose the rail interval layout for longer interpretation and the wide layout for short notes.
Flow direction changes the composition; vertical supports up to three steps, horizontal up to five.
Matrices accept three to six columns/rows, with explicit column weights and alignment. Their full
and compact variants change density. Roadmap phase widths reflect positive durations; activities
match phases and gate captions have their own reserved region below the track rules.

Containers own small labels and numbered nodes. Center circle text horizontally and vertically with
explicit insets; inspect actual glyphs. Labels share geometry with their background. Never repair
overflow by shrinking type or suppressing legitimate content. Split, rewrite or choose a supported
composition when the capacity check fails. These checks estimate capacity, not exact font metrics.

The reference supplies identity and page roles, not a pixel-perfect target. This implementation uses
readable secondary text, a slightly lighter mint, balanced line breaks and explicit bottom spacing.
Arial replaces the reference Latin face; Arial Unicode MS is the tested CJK choice on the validation
machine. Fonts are not embedded or distributed. Other systems require actual regular/bold rendering
checks; a successful font lookup alone does not establish appearance or PowerPoint parity.

Full-deck rhythm alternates opening/summary, evidence, method and close. `meta.designScope: "excerpt"`
supports standalone evidence excerpts. Layout selection is deterministic, constrained by content fit
and recent geometry; it does not invent content or produce unbounded layouts. This is a creation
adapter, not a verified arbitrary-PPTX refurbishment workflow.
