# Violet Momentum

Violet Momentum is a reusable operational editorial system. Electric Violet, white paper,
near-black statements, open rules and aligned evidence preserve the reference identity.
`design.json` is the numerical source of truth. Discover fields and capacities with the
standard `design.mjs` and `slots.mjs` commands; supply content, never page coordinates.

## Composition and content

Ten roles retain all eight reference responsibilities and add a violet chapter and position
ratings. Eighteen registered compositions vary actual geometry. `auto` resolves content fit
and recent composition deterministically. Explicit layout IDs must also fit. A complete deck
opens with `violetCoverSlide` and ends with `violetActionsSlide`; use
`meta.designScope: "excerpt"` for extracts. The split cover supports three or four metrics;
with three metrics, the last metric occupies the entire lower row. Action `stack` accommodates
three phases; four phases require the timeline. Generic slot budgets aid discovery; resolved
text frames decide actual capacity. Overflow fails before rendering. Reorganize or split content.

Version 0.2 separates cover quantities from their unit-bearing labels, uses 28pt key values,
14pt body and 11.5pt supporting evidence, and keeps the 25pt content title scale. Native tables
share a 0.12in horizontal cell inset with their fitted plans. Only the actual-value column is
violet and bold; this is reading hierarchy, not an inferred status. Repeated takeaways use a
quieter 12pt role. Action requests use the same violet decision role in both compositions.

All assertions, labels, units, periods, sources, targets, status text, thresholds, causes,
owners and dates belong to content. The kit does not infer status or impose investment,
financial or operational policy. The summary's gap is arithmetic actual minus target; status
is caller-authored. Supply `gapUnit` for percent-point differences or independently defined
gap units; the literal native scorecard accepts caller-authored values. Every example is synthetic.

## Native evidence and editing

The scorecard and summary are native PowerPoint tables. The trend is a native single-series
column chart and retains signed, zero and tiny values. Data labels use integer, decimal or
scientific formatting without changing values. Tiny values may be subpixel on a common axis.
Scientific formats can display differently between canonical and PowerPoint renderers;
the raw source values remain intact. Verify displayed precision for the intended audience.
The first implementation covers observed values only; forecasts and multiseries charts are
not registered. All columns use the same violet. Inflections and reporting boundaries are
editable annotations derived from the numeric source. Canonical plot insets were measured
for the tested renderer; PowerPoint plot internals require separate visual review.

Progress tracks, position dots, convergence leaders and total bubbles are native editable
shapes. Progress is computed from the same input percentage; zero has no fake minimum fill.
Exactly one of ten rating dots is filled, expressing a position rather than cumulative area.
The visible `scaleLabel` (default `Position · 1–10`) and 1/10 endpoints explain that encoding.
Projects expose a `progressLabel` (default `Completion`) above their progress tracks; health
status remains independently supplied. Override these optional text slots for the deck's
language and measurement definition. A 100% completed activity can still require corrective
action; the kit never turns progress into a success judgment.
Attribution drivers must sum to the declared total, including signed and zero contributions.
The total circle keeps a constant area and owns its centered number and smaller unit in one
native shape. Changing a chart or cell manually does not synchronize independent annotations,
progress shapes, derived gaps or total bubbles. Regenerate from content to synchronize them.

## Reference decisions and limits

The source image contains eight 16:9 data pages and no photographs. Its split cover and white
action ending take precedence over contradictory photo-cover and black-closing descriptions.
The design specification explicitly permits white data slides without suitable photographs;
no photo quota, image ingestion or unrelated placeholder illustration is introduced.

The canvas scales the source's 10×5.625in to 13.333×7.5in. Readable text is larger and darker
than the source's small gray captions. Installed Tahoma regular/bold retains the source's
Latin family; Noto Sans SC supplies Chinese and mixed text because MiSans and Quattrocento
Sans are unavailable here. Fonts are not bundled. Upright regular and bold rendering must
be rechecked on other machines. No arbitrary existing-deck refurbishment, animation, asset
preservation or cross-platform PowerPoint equivalence is claimed.
