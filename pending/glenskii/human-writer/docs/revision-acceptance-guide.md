# Revision Acceptance Guide

Use this guide after a substantial edit and before returning the draft.

## Accept the revision only when

- The writer's facts, point of view, and level of certainty remain intact.
- Every substantial change has a clear editorial reason.
- The draft reads naturally without becoming bland or over-smoothed.
- The formatting still matches the original document unless format was part of the requested repair.

## Return a note instead of making the change when

- The draft needs new facts, examples, or a stronger argument.
- A wording choice may be deliberate but cannot be verified from the surrounding text.
- The requested outcome conflicts with preserving the writer's voice.

For a formal handoff, use the bundled revision-note template. Keep the note short and state only the categories of changes made.
