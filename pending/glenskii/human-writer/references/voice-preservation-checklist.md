# Voice Preservation Checklist

Use this checklist before returning a substantial revision. It protects the writer's point of view while allowing necessary mechanical repairs.

## Check the draft against the source

- Keep the writer's position, certainty, and emotional temperature unchanged.
- Keep deliberate fragments, colloquial language, and short sentences when they suit the voice.
- Do not replace a specific detail with a broader, smoother statement.
- Do not add a conclusion, transition, or call to action that the writer did not supply.

## Check individual changes

- Prefer a local repair over a sentence rewrite.
- Keep the original rhythm where a sentence is already clear.
- Remove repetition only when it is accidental, not when it provides emphasis.
- Flag a factual uncertainty instead of silently changing it.

## Return decision

Return the revision when every change can be explained as a correction of a machine-written pattern, grammar, clarity, or consistency issue. If a change alters meaning, restore the original wording and flag the concern separately.
