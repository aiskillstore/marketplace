# Nex HOA Management Pack

> Free skill by [Nex AI](https://nex-ai.be). This file is the public guide from [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/skills/nex-hoa-management-pack). Scripts, extension source, and templates from the original package are not in this folder.

## What it does

- Generates compliant meeting notices, agendas and minutes for board and annual member meetings.
- Builds the annual budget worksheet with operating lines, reserve contribution and a dues calculation per unit.
- Produces the violation notice ladder: courtesy letter, formal notice, hearing notice, fine assessment.
- Drafts dues collection letters through the escalation path up to lien referral, with the disclaimer to route to counsel.

## Use cases

- A self-managed HOA board handling its own paperwork
- Preparing an annual meeting packet and budget
- Documenting a covenant violation properly before enforcement
- Standardising dues reminders and delinquency escalation

## Known limitations

US HOA practice only, and state condominium and HOA acts differ widely. Output is a working draft, not legal advice, and enforcement or lien steps should always be reviewed by association counsel.

## Example

**Request**

Draft a formal notice of violation for a homeowner in Florida who has an unapproved fence, following the four-step escalation ladder.

**Result**

**FORMAL NOTICE OF VIOLATION**
Assoc: Sunset Lakes HOA (FL)
Violation: Unapproved Perimeter Fence
Provision: CC&Rs Art. IV, Sec 2.
Action: You have 14 days to submit an ARC request or remove the structure. Failure to cure may lead to a hearing and fines. Check FL Stat. 720 for rights.

## Questions

### Does this provide legal advice?

No. It drafts paperwork based on the documents and state statutes you provide, but all outputs should be reviewed by association counsel before being sent or recorded.

### How does it handle different state laws?

The skill includes a 'first-check rule' to verify state statutes and association bylaws before drafting any notices or minutes to ensure compliance with local requirements.

### What specific documents can it generate?

It handles board meeting minutes, violation notice ladders, assessment collection letters, annual meeting packages (proxies/ballots), and architectural review forms.
