## The Complete Document Engine for Belgian Property Managers

Managing a *Vereniging van Mede-Eigenaars* (VME) in Belgium requires strict adherence to Book 3 of the Civil Code (Art. 3.84 - 3.100). One procedural error—like a missing majority type or an incorrect notice period—can lead to voided decisions by a Justice of the Peace. The **Nex Syndicus VME Pack** automates the generation of legally compliant documents, ensuring every general meeting and financial statement stands up to legal scrutiny.

## What it does

This skill acts as a specialized document factory for professional and voluntary syndici. It generates the entire lifecycle of VME documentation in Dutch or English, including:

- **Meeting Invitations:** Enforcing the 15-day legal notice and mandatory agenda items.
- **Agendas:** Automatically assigning the correct voting majority (1/2, 2/3, 4/5, or unanimity) per decision type.
- **Minutes (Notulen):** Creating structured tables to record votes and names of dissenters, compliant with Art. 3.93 BW.
- **Annual Accounts (Jaarafrekening):** Correctly splitting working capital from reserve capital with the mandatory 5% minimum contribution calculations.
- **Debt Collection:** Standardized formal notices (ingebrekestellingen) for overdue contributions.

## Why use this skill?

Unlike a generic AI, this skill "knows" Belgian property law. It handles edge cases like the reduction of voting power when one owner holds more than 50% of the shares and calculates quorums automatically. It saves hours of manual drafting while significantly reducing the risk of 4-month challenge periods by disgruntled co-owners.
