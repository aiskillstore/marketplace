> Free skill by [Nex AI](https://nex-ai.be). This file is the public guide from [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/skills/nex-ai-literacy-pack). Scripts, extension source, and templates from the original package are not in this folder.

## The problem

Article 4 of the EU AI Act mandates AI literacy training for all staff as of February 2025, but most companies lack the structured documentation to prove compliance. Using ChatGPT or Copilot without a formal policy and inventory creates civil liability and regulatory risks under the new market surveillance standards.

## What it does

- Generates a comprehensive AI system inventory with risk-class triage (prohibited, high-risk, transparency, minimal).
- Drafts a customized internal AI policy covering acceptable use, data privacy rules, and approval flows.
- Produces a role-based training structure with specialized tracks for management, general staff, and technical teams.
- Assembles a complete Article 4 compliance file including attendance templates, oversight logs, and review cycles.
- Flags specific high-risk systems against the Digital Omnibus timeline and Annex III requirements.

## Frameworks & tools

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), Digital Omnibus on AI updates (June 2026), Annex I, and Annex III safety standards.

## Why this beats prompting it yourself

General prompts fail to capture the specific multi-track training requirements and risk triage nuances demanded by Article 4. This skill ensures your documentation survives a regulatory audit by linking the inventory directly to policy strictness and training depth, reflecting the latest 2026 legal updates.

## Use cases

- Drafting a defensible AI literacy package for an organization using generative AI tools.
- Productizing AI compliance as a fixed-price service for legal or consultancy clients.
- Conducting a risk-based audit of "shadow AI" and creating an approved tool register.
- Preparing specialized human oversight documentation for high-risk AI deployments.

## Known limitations

Does not provide official EU certification. Requires legal review for specific local labor laws or works council requirements such as CAO 39.
