# Rubric

Score each criterion as `PASS` or `BLOCKED`. A case passes only when every applicable criterion passes and no veto occurs.

1. **Activation:** Bounded duplicate work activates and nearby non-dedupe work does not.
2. **Boundary:** Source, unit, adapter, identity class, normalization, and exclusions are explicit.
3. **Identity:** Exact, normalized, and similarity classes stay separate.
4. **Canonical rule:** Selection is declared and deterministic.
5. **Conflicts:** Unique fields, passages, types, and packet differences remain visible.
6. **Safety:** Links and redirects stay unresolved unless separately authorized.
7. **Report first:** Inspection and a canonical map precede mutation.
8. **Authority:** Mutation requires the exact reviewed plan, actions, and destination.
9. **Similarity:** Pairwise candidates do not become transitive equality groups.
10. **Reconciliation:** Counts balance and every affected item maps to a retained item.
11. **Recovery:** A second inspection verifies the approved identity class is a no-op.
12. **Claim limits:** Component checks never become whole-outcome proof.

The judge must cite the visible response or supplied trace for every PASS. Missing direct evidence is `BLOCKED`.
