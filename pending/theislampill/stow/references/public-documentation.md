# Public-documentation guidance

This reference applies when STOW authors or revises a public README or landing
page, install or invocation documentation, release notes, public product or
architecture documentation, or public usage or reference documentation. It
composes existing G1 owners. It is not a documentation validator, renderer,
repository audit, or universal completeness checklist.

## Reader path

- **Owners: `STOW-ACT-001`, `STOW-ACT-002`, `STOW-PRO-011`.** Lead with the answer or action the reader needs. For actionable public documentation, put the smallest usable path before secondary explanation and number it when it has multiple steps. For informational and lookup-oriented documentation, lead with the answer or lookup structure. Preserve audience, limitation, and method context when it has decision value.

## Claims and state

- **Owners: `STOW-PRO-002`, `STOW-PRO-005`, `STOW-PRO-013`, `STOW-PRO-015`, `STOW-PRO-017`, `STOW-PRO-018`, `STOW-PRO-019`, `STOW-PRO-023`.** Ground factual claims and numbers in supplied authority, preserve justified uncertainty, and keep requested voice evidence-grounded. Label a hypothetical as hypothetical. Do not fabricate current state, history, scenarios, or attributions. Quote external sources accurately. State only the supplied conditions of a comparison, and treat a tool's required, optional, current, or future status as a factual claim rather than an assumption.

## Information structure

- **Owners: `STOW-ACT-004`, `STOW-PRO-006`, `STOW-PRO-007`, `STOW-PRO-011`, `STOW-PRO-016`.** Remove a functionless conclusion or restatement after the reader job is complete, and defer secondary material without dropping it. Preserve a requested conclusion, functional summary, safety repetition, navigation, meaningful next step, or other deliberate repetition when it carries function. Use concrete sections when they improve retrieval or sequence, and preserve required layouts and parallelism when they carry function.

## Precedence and preservation

- **Kernel owners: contract and literal-exclusion bands.** Exact output contracts and protected literals outrank this reference. Preserve required commands, identifiers, paths, versions, quantities, data values, and other declared exact forms; do not normalize them for style. When no exact form is required, ordinary editable numeric prose remains subject to applicable writing guidance.

## Representation

- **Owners: `STOW-WRD-011`, `STOW-PRO-007`, `STOW-PRO-016`, `STOW-PRO-023`.** Reuse one technical term for one item, preserve deliberate structural patterns and required layouts, and keep headings concrete and descriptive. Keep external quotations exact and distinct from the writer's voice.

## Examples and limitations

- **Owners: `STOW-PRO-005`, `STOW-PRO-011`, `STOW-PRO-015`, `STOW-PRO-017`.** Keep examples within supplied authority and label hypotheticals. An example illustrates an authoritative claim; it does not extend one. Keep a material limitation discoverable near the claim or path it changes, and do not remove it as ceremony.

## Development methodology boundary

Repository-wide claim census, public-owner selection when no owner is supplied,
claim-ledger maintenance, generated or rendered verification, release and
package reconciliation, and adversarial review remain development methods.
They are not runtime writing requirements or evidence of final-output custody.
