# Healthcare controls and sources

Primary sources checked 2026-11-04. Verify current local privacy, advertising and professional standards before launch.

- [HHS HIPAA marketing guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/marketing/index.html) explains use/disclosure of protected health information for marketing and distinctions/exceptions. Covered-entity context, communication purpose and remuneration matter. Obtain privacy review; neither all patient messages nor all service information has one classification.
- [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) addresses express and implied advertising claims and scientific substantiation. Review the overall consumer impression, not only literal wording. The needed evidence depends on the claim; a disclaimer does not substitute for substantiation.

These US sources do not resolve rules for other countries, state consumer-health laws, minors, prescription-drug advertising or professional licensing. Retrieve the relevant official source and identify unresolved scope. Treat tracking technologies as a separate privacy review; do not assume all public-page visitors are patients or that all public-page tracking is harmless.

Approval packet: audience and purpose, claims with medical reviewer/evidence, patient-story permission if any, precise data fields and recipients, intake versus marketing boundary, message channel, retention and access controls, opt-outs, incident/escalation owner and live-test evidence. Do not include actual patient information in the packet unless necessary and authorized for its recipients.
