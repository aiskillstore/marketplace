# Financial controls and sources

Public primary sources checked 2026-11-04. Recheck the exact jurisdiction, regulator, product and current rule before approving live material.

- [SEC investment-adviser marketing guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing): the US adviser rule addresses misleading advertisements, performance presentation and conditions around testimonials/endorsements. It does not govern every financial business. Do not reuse a prohibited/conditional performance format merely because a competitor used it.
- [SEC staff marketing FAQs](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/marketing-compliance-frequently-asked-questions): inspect applicable performance details and current interpretations before using gross/net, hypothetical or extracted results. Do not certify an advertisement from a summary.
- [FTC GLBA privacy guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/how-comply-privacy-consumer-financial-information-rule-gramm-leach-bliley-act): financial privacy obligations depend on entity/activity, relationship and disclosure. Marketing consent and a privacy notice are not interchangeable permissions. Determine the applicable regulator rather than assuming FTC jurisdiction over every institution.

For lending, deposit, insurance, securities or cross-border promotions, retrieve the specific current regulator's advertising, disclosure, fair-lending and privacy requirements. Do not treat US examples as global rules. Preserve required audit copies in the approved system, minimizing unrelated personal information.

Review packet: exact audience and channel, claims and substantiation, product terms/date, readable disclosures, affiliate/referral relationships, data recipients, consent scope, reviewer and expiry/re-review trigger. Never share customer financial data with an analyzer, CRM, ad network or partner merely to improve targeting.
