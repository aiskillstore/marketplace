# Campaigns and copy

## Pick the commercial job

- Newsletter: useful education for the subscribed topic; measure qualified return visits or relevant product actions, not just opens.
- Feature release: explain who benefits, the job improved, eligibility/plan limits, and one next action. Suppress people without access or give truthful upgrade context. Do not announce unshipped features as available.
- Webinar/demo: align topic to use case and buying stage; state verified date, time zone, host, and attendance terms. Suppress registrants from repeated acquisition invites and route reminders separately.
- Trial conversion: emphasize value realized or the next useful milestone. Use verified trial end and current billing state, not generic manufactured urgency.
- Upgrade/annual plan: target the relevant decision-maker and verified unmet need. State approved plan, price, currency, billing period, discount duration, and renewal basis where applicable. Do not imply annual prepayment is monthly billing.
- Re-engagement: offer a reason tied to the user's prior job or subscribed interest; sunset persistent nonresponse using reliable engagement signals and applicable eligibility.

## Shape the email

Start with a specific user problem or earned benefit, give a short explanation, and end with one main CTA. Use multiple links only when they support the same purpose or the requested newsletter format. Match CTA to the actual landing page and access state. Use an honest subject/preheader pair; do not fake replies, account alerts, personal correspondence, scarcity, or negative consequences.

Use plan-aware and role-aware copy. Prefer "Create your first report" to "Finish setup" only if that action exists and matters. Never expose private account usage in a shared subject line. Safe fallback: omit an unknown name rather than emit broken syntax. Avoid provider-specific merge syntax until verified.

## Campaign brief

Document purpose, hypothesis, audience/exclusions, verified product facts, proof available, offer restrictions, requested content, send window/time zone, conflicts with active flows, primary conversion event, denominator/window, guardrails, and approval state. A calendar should state audience and objective per send, with collision handling and a reason for proposed cadence. Do not fill a calendar with promotions solely to meet a volume target.

## Copy-only requests

Return the requested copy directly, with a brief assumption or material blocker only if needed. No forced stack recommendation, strategy expansion, or new-tool upsell.
