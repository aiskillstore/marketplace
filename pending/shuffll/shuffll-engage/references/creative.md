# Creative journeys and finishing

Use this guidance only for journey and finishing operations exposed by the current connection. Load their actual schemas, available catalogs and saved journey state before choosing inputs. Public availability varies by organization and release.

- Choose suitable authorized background music by default, matching the story and tone. Respect silence and explicit track choices. Keep dialogue clear by lowering or ducking music beneath speech. Discover tracks through `catalogs_music` where exposed; a track's presence in a catalog does not apply it to a timeline.
- Prefer natural, real-time movement. Explicitly discourage slow motion in generation prompts unless the user requests it or approves a specific artistic exception.
- Keep Seedance's generated picture and paired speech audio together. If replacement TTS is requested, explain the mouth-timing limitation and offer separate narration through supported operations; do not treat it as a synchronized replacement performance. Actor captions must follow the actual paired performance; do not invent words or timestamps from the intended script.
- Watch and listen before trimming. Preserve every spoken word and protected action. Source-bound word timestamps can establish speech bounds, but do not establish continuity or dramatic timing. If evidence is missing, preserve the full paired clip and state the limitation.
- Use restrained, immediate timeline-native transitions only when the current catalog exposes the same effect for the actual scene shape and it has verified interactive playback and final Editor Worker MP4 rendering. Verification evidence must apply to that effect, scene shape and implementation/release; catalog presence alone is insufficient. An empty catalog means cuts. Never offer or generate AI transitions, FFmpeg-rendered transition assets, bridges, or fallback transition jobs. Do not infer that Blur, Push or Zoom are available from a style name in a schema. Keep straight cuts where dialogue, continuity or comedy benefit.
- Use editable overlays only through available operations such as `journey_overlays_save/get`. Read the current typography, artboard and choreography schemas. Keep text readable, leave caption space, avoid covering faces, allow longer personalized text and RTL, and preserve stable layer IDs and unrelated bindings during edits. An overlay animation is not a scene transition.

If `journey_preview_generate` exposes `finishing`, use its current inputs to apply authorized choices; do not invent parameters from this guide. When available, inspect saved choices through `journey_preview_inspection_get`, then inspect the actual preview. Music, trimming, overlays, captions and transitions each need final export verification before a final-quality claim. Reuse compatible owned assets rather than regenerating them without need or authorization.
