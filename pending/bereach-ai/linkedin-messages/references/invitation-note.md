# The invitation note

Framework: premise (Becc Holland), the only note. One call per lead, only when the lead has a real fact
under 6 months (something they wrote, shared or engaged with). No real fact: no note. Free accounts: 3
notes a month (`notes`).
Why: a stranger accepts for a reason about them, and a note asks nothing.
Parts: 1. their real fact, a post named by what it said (its month only when natural); 2. who writes, in
a few words, said as what they do for people like the reader, never a verb about connecting tools; 3. why
that fact made them want to connect. Each part names something concrete, never "the topic".
Shape: "Bonjour {Prénom}, [their fact]. [Who writes], [why that made me want to connect]."
Each note is written for its person with their real first name in place of `{Prénom}`: no blank ever reaches a draft. Never claim a shared trade the facts do not show. Vary the closing
line between notes.
Size: 200 characters (or `notes.maxLength`), red over. Never an offer or a question in a note.
Never: a pitch, a link, a product name, a category label, a generic compliment in place of the why.
