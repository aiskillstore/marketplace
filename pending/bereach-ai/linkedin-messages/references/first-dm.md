# The first message after they accept

When: approved once the person reads `connected` in `bereach_contacts` (`campaignId` of the list,
`outreachStatus: "connected"`) (SKILL.md, Step 5). Approved earlier, the line sends it after the
accept, usually within an hour or two, which reads automated. Waiting 1 to 3 days after the accept is
practice, not measured.

Framework: Premise first with the user's real offer (Becc Holland; reciprocity: Regan 1971, Falk 2007).
Why: the parts are what is measured: a real reason about them, their problem rather than a pitch, one
easy specific ask. A gift they keep raises replies in field trials; a meeting ask lowers them.
Parts:
1. About them: the lead's fact or the persona's moment line, plus one clause on why it matters to them.
   After a note, it never opens on the note's fact or moment line: it opens on something else, a second
   fact, the consequence of the note's moment for them, or the reason for the offer.
2. Who writes, in ONE clause under 15 words inside a sentence about them: what the user does for this
   reader, in their words; never a list of features or a verb about connecting tools, never the same
   clause or closing words twice in one batch.
3. One yes/no question offering a real offer (SKILL.md, The offer), its object in full words, tied to
   line 1; else one question about their fact.
Shape: "Hi [Name], [their fact], and [what that means for them]. I'm [name] and I [what I do for people
like them]. Shall I send you [the thing they keep, for their fact]?"
Size: 2 to 4 full sentences, as long as the three parts need. Shorter: drop a whole idea, never shrink
each part. Proof waits for follow-up 1.

The offer per persona: one kind per persona, only a real one (SKILL.md, The offer). Its object is who this lead sells to, read from their profile or company page; not readable: the card's offer word for word, never a guess. A sample uses only what they show in public. Never a number guessed from headcount. Contacts come with why each one fits, and never
"they are yours" before a yes. An agency gets leads for its clients, not for itself. A yes to contacts: `bereach_find_people` with
`save: "skip"`, the people shown to the user before anything goes, then `bereach_send_dm` with one line
on why each fits.

Languages: French with a space before "?" and "!", no English idiom translated; German "Sie" by default.
Every language says the same offer, the same ask and the same "free".
