# Persona card template

One card per persona, in the reader's words where you have them. Cards are short: a seller writes a message
from them in one read. Leave a field empty rather than invent it: a precise card claims you know that precisely.

## The words

ICP, segment, plan group, persona, buying group, Why now, signal, `whyNow`: the glossary "The words" in the
linkedin-outreach-method skill's `references/icp.md`, the one definition. Fit decides who is in; timing decides the order.

## ICP, plan groups and cards

- The ICP is the company half of a plan segment: its kind of company, size and place stay in the search plan.
- Each plan group goes into the Found by of one card; groups that would get the same first message share one
  card, even across segments. Roles the plan guessed end in `(guess)`.
- One title can split into two cards when the situation differs. Two kinds of buyer (an agency and a
  freelancer, a firm and a solo) never share a card.

## Core (every card, 7 lines)

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Name and Found by** | The company first (kind, size, the situation that makes it a fit), then the role, never a first name; its short name (`network-manager`, used in the tag `persona:<short-name>`); then 3 to 6 titles a profile shows, copied from the plan's groups, and the ICP by name |
| **Why now** | What pushes them to act now, at the user's hiring step or just before, ONE window (6 months at most), and the public fact that shows it on a profile or post (the signal to look for). What the event changes for them, never the event or title back to them |
| **Pain** | The push as a moment of their work, one sentence, with its source. Never "many firms do X" |
| **Offer they keep** | From the user's real offers or a prospect's ask only, else empty. Never a meeting, never what this persona sells, never the Proof retold, never a third party's data. An agency gets leads for its clients, not for itself |
| **Proof that lands** | One real case in the user's words and what changed, with its source, else "None yet" |
| **Objection** | The likeliest resistance (habit, anxiety, not my call) and the user's one-line answer in their words, else an empty text field |
| **Tone and never say** | Tu or vous and first name or not (the Voice card's form; none yet, vous where a formal form exists), 2 to 3 words to avoid. May add the awareness level in one line: knows the problem (a cold reader's default) or compares tools (only when replies or posts show it) |

A line that is not the user's words or a reply ends in `(guess)`: never in quotation marks, never stated as
fact in a message. A line becomes `seen in N replies` only from 2 replies saying the same in meaning, each
wording quoted; a politeness reply never counts. No tool, date, deadline or change over time that the facts
do not give.

## More (only when known, shown collapsed)

Today they use (and the one compatibility question, for a software seller), Who else decides, What they do
with the offer, What it costs (the user's price line), Goals, Busy months, Their words (from replies only),
and Not this persona: who looks similar but is not (students, side projects, the sender's own team), held,
never written to.

## Rules

- Pain is not priority. A pain your product solves may not be what they are spending on this quarter.
- A row with the same value on every card is said once above the cards, not on each.
- Two personas that would get the same first message are one: merge them.
- Tu or vous: mirror their reply, and check it in the results by persona.

## Example (fictional seller: field calibration software for water networks)

| Field | Water utility with ageing meters: its network manager | Engineering consultancy on water projects: its project director |
|---|---|---|
| Name and Found by | `network-manager`: Network Manager, Head of Water Networks, Distribution Manager (ICP: water utilities and municipal water services, 50 to 2,000 staff) | `consultancy`: Project Director, Head of Water, Founder (ICP: engineering consultancies in water) |
| Why now | A meter renewal tender (6 months): accuracy to prove before renewing. Signal: a tender published (guess) | A network study won: a model to calibrate before delivery. Signal: a new study announced (guess) |
| Pain | Our meters drift and we find out at the yearly audit (guess) | The field data is too thin to calibrate the model (guess) |
| Offer they keep | A drift check on one district's meters (the user offers it today) | Empty: ask the user |
| Proof that lands | Drift found on one district before the yearly audit, for a water utility (unnamed; your words) | None yet |
| Objection | Not now (no tender yet); answer: empty, the user writes it | We already have a hydraulic model; answer: one district calibrated, fixed price (your words) |
| Tone and never say | Vous; never "disruptive", "solution". Knows the problem | Vous, tu if a founder posts informally; never "game-changer", "synergy" |
