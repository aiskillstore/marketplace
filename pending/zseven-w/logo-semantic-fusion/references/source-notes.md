## High-level synthesis

The recurring design sequence is:

1. reject literal juxtaposition, stacking, and narrative illustration;
2. decompose meanings into contours, strokes, voids, modules, actions, and language structures;
3. locate one compatible structural primitive;
4. redraw the ideas as one dependent geometry;
5. remove detail until the fusion survives without explanation;
6. validate the geometry before using color or mockups.

Small-size testing, optical correction, similarity review, script-safety checks, and production handoff requirements are independent professional extensions added by this skill.
