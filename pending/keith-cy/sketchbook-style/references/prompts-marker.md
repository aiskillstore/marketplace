# Prompts behind the kept samples — variant marker (速写本 · 马克笔)

Every sample: Qwen-Image 2.1 Q6 on ComfyUI, euler / simple, 20 steps, CFG 1.0, 1024×1024, then `scripts/inkpost.py` with the options shown.

Iterations: m1 pilot asked for "bold solid black fills on dark parts, openings and screens" → coverage 0.45–0.60, lino-print look, a floor grid (rejected). m2 switched to "only one or two dark parts filled solid black … most surfaces left white" + "no floor" (A/B/C kept). m3 full set; its D page still fell into rows. m4 asked for "one dense overlapping cluster on the left … never in rows" for D, and redid the person.
