# Prompts behind the kept samples — variant ink (速写本 · 浓墨)

Every sample: Qwen-Image 2.1 Q6 on ComfyUI, euler / simple, 20 steps, CFG 1.0, 1024×1024, then `scripts/inkpost.py` with the options shown.

Iterations: k1 "large solid black areas … bold black silhouette" → coverage 0.70 / solid 0.93, closing in post swallowed the reversed whites, hearts came back on the notes page (rejected). k2 "about half of the object is solid black … the lit side stays white", notes "plain words with arrows and nothing else", `--heavy` without closing (helmet and console kept). k3 full set, plus "no cast shadow on the ground"; its D pages became dense heaps. k4 D redone spread out.
