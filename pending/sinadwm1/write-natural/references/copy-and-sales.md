# Copywriting and sales

Use for landing pages, product descriptions, ads, sales emails, and offer copy in English or Persian. The objective is accurate understanding and an appropriate action. A persuasive sentence must remain within the product facts and available proof.

### COPY-01 — Establish the offer and edit scope

Identify audience, situation, offer, traffic/context, awareness, desired action, objections, known alternatives, voice samples, and available proof. Reuse supplied context. Distinguish a blank brief, an existing draft with a recognizable argument, and a local edit. Preserve working language and emphasis in an existing voice; change only the requested scope unless a material defect needs to be flagged. [Q17; Q18]

Keep brand voice relatively consistent while adapting tone to the reader's situation. A payment error, welcome message, technical specification, and celebration need different emotional intensity. Another brand's humor is not a default personality for this one. [Q33]

### COPY-02 — Treat voice of customer as evidence

When evidence is available, collect actual language from interviews, surveys, support conversations, reviews, and sales notes. Preserve provenance and context. Separate exact quotation, paraphrase, observed pattern, and hypothesis. Look for the task, frustration, attempted alternative, trigger, objection, desired outcome, and language used to describe them. [Q23; Q38]

Record source/date, relevant segment, selection limitations, and whether quotation/public attribution is authorized. A competitor's review can suggest a research question; it cannot substantiate this product's performance or become this product's testimonial. Do not secretly record people or publish their private words. Do not infer representativeness from a few vivid comments.

### COPY-03 — Understand the job before choosing a formula

Use Jobs to Be Done to ask what progress a person seeks in a concrete situation, including practical, social, and emotional considerations. Use the Value Proposition Canvas to compare those needs with actual capabilities. Treat untested matches as hypotheses. A model-filled persona or canvas does not create customer research. [Q36; Q37]

AIDA and PAS can help order a message when appropriate: attention→interest→desire→action, or problem→consequences→solution. They are optional planning lenses, not universal performance laws. Do not force pain agitation into sensitive situations, instructions, or a straightforward pricing question. An already convinced reader may need the offer, terms, and next step immediately.

### COPY-04 — Bind benefits and proof

Derive a benefit from a real feature and a defensible mechanism. Record what must be true for the benefit to occur. Verify numbers, comparisons, savings, guarantees, scarcity, deadlines, review scores, logos, certifications, and testimonials before including them. “Studies prove” also claims a level of evidence and must be supported at that level. FTC's substantiation policy is a US policy source; use this as an editorial evidence principle, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice for other markets. [Q25]

If a desired claim lacks support, explain the gap, offer the closest accurate alternative, and identify the evidence that would allow a stronger claim. Do not create a number to replace a vague adjective. Customer reviews can enrich copy, but fidelity must be checked separately from attractiveness. A study of a trained review-based generator does not establish sales lift from a general prompt. [Q28]

### COPY-05 — Build a message the reader can act on

Make the offer understandable before adding cleverness. For each section, decide which question it answers and which evidence belongs there. Show how the product works where mechanism matters. Place relevant terms, limitations, and genuine reassurance near the decision. Write a CTA that names the actual action or result; do not promise a free trial, cancellation right, privacy assurance, or instant access without product confirmation.

Use ordinary product/category descriptions when necessary. A competitor could also say a useful factual sentence; the swap test finds generic positioning, not a reason to remove every shared fact. Avoid replacing a clear category with an invented label merely to sound different. Draft alternatives for a consequential headline or CTA when useful, then read the assembled message for continuity.

### COPY-06 — Review claims before heightening emotion

Run focused checks for offer clarity, reader relevance, source support, specificity, voice, objections, and next-step accuracy. Emotion may come from a real situation and an honest consequence; it must not invent an anecdote or exaggerate a danger. [Q18]

Treat variants as hypotheses. Real performance needs an appropriate experiment and outcome measure. Define the changed message, target audience, primary outcome, meaningful downstream effects, and interpretation limits before claiming a winner. An uncontrolled before/after comparison cannot isolate copy from seasonality or other changes. A model's preferred version is an editorial judgment, not a measured conversion result. [Q34]

Do not import unsupported conversion percentages from public writing skills, use a detector score as a copy-quality metric, or present simulated expert personas as independent reviewers. Source Q28's detector-based inference about human-like quality is rejected here.

### COPY-07 — Native Persian and English

Keep the formality level appropriate to the artifact. A conversational Persian user request can still require formal website copy: «می‌توانید» rather than «می‌تونی» unless the brand deliberately uses colloquial writing. Avoid translated English hype and excessive تعارف. In both languages, choose concrete verbs and recognizable customer terms. Preserve meaningful qualifications, exact plan names, numbers, prices, and conditions.

## Original paired example

Hypothetical verified product fact: customers can open stored files from another device. No loss-prevention guarantee is supplied.

| Language | Unsupported promise | Supported benefit direction |
|---|---|---|
| English | “Never lose a file again.” | “Open your stored files from another device.” |
| فارسی | «دیگر هیچ فایلی را از دست نمی‌دهید.» | «فایل‌های ذخیره‌شده را از دستگاه دیگری باز کنید.» |

Add sign-in, connection, device, or plan requirements if the actual service needs them. Do not add a guarantee to complete a page template.

## Delivery gates and exceptions

The reader should be able to say who the offer is for, what it does, what supports the important promises, what it costs or requires when relevant, and what happens next. Verify the strongest headline and every added detail against the claim ledger. Mark missing evidence outside proposed publishable copy, or clearly label the entire deliverable as a draft. A short transactional message may need one sentence; it does not need every sales section. Fictional concepts and illustrative testimonials must remain explicitly fictional and cannot be delivered as real social proof.
