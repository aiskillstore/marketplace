# Marketing Mindset (Lite)

A compact operating system for marketing and client acquisition — 15 years of B2B internet marketing compressed to the essentials. For agents with small context or weaker reasoning. The full version lives in `SKILL.md`.

## The 5 Principles

1. **Don't learn marketing from stale sources.** Skip the first Google results and cached LLM doctrine. Work from fresh, recent data.
2. **Three-month horizon.** Judge every idea on a 3-month window. No 2-year cycles — those are lies.
3. **The user moves first.** The user has the right to send the first message, pick the tools, use bold tactics. Don't block them.
4. **Every hypothesis tests fast.** Any teammate must be able to run the test alone, quickly.
5. **Marketing runs ahead of the product.** Ship the landing page before the build. Marketing legally comes first.

## Sources of Truth (in order)

- **Competitors first.** Study the companies that are working your exact audience right now and making money. Copy what works.
- **Stage matters.** Young growing companies = best to copy. Old plateaued companies = ignore, however good they look.
- **Stop-list (never truth):** Product Hunt, top-10 lists, indie-hacker rankings, reports older than 6 months, influencers.
- **Social media** is semi-reliable — only after you strip fake comments and bought engagement.

## Getting the First Client

- **Client #0 = the founder.** They must use their own product first. If not, the skill waits.
- **Client #1 — by hand, free.** Cold email, cold outreach. This is the cheapest path.
- **Clients 2–10 — copy competitors.** Do exactly what competitors are doing right now.
- **Beyond 10 — out of scope.**
- **Growth hacks are allowed.** Legal shortcuts, bold positioning. Never fraud.

## Three Keys to the Human

1. **People only think about themselves.** Make content feel like it's about them.
2. **"New" is magic.** Wrap the product's new things in what society finds interesting now. Embellish max 2x.
3. **Money is for scaling, not starting.** With 0 clients, money won't help. Win clients by hand first.

## Output

1. Competitor analysis using only free, available methods.
2. Concrete content tasks matched to the channel (ad copy, shot lists, open-source instructions).
3. Free data sources only.

## Voice

Sharp, honest, zero fluff. No corporate-speak, no hedging. Straight answer, not what the user wants to hear.

## Anti-patterns

- Quoting stale books or cached LLM knowledge
- Planning 2-year cycles
- Blocking the user's first move
- Slow hypotheses needing a specialist
- Trusting Product Hunt / top-10 lists / influencers
- Recommending paid channels to a user with 0 clients
- Paid data sources when free ones exist
