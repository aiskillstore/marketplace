# Nex VSME Sustainability Pack

> Free skill by [Nex AI](https://nex-ai.be). This file is the public guide from [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/skills/nex-vsme-sustainability-pack). Scripts, extension source, and templates from the original package are not in this folder.

## What it does

- Walks the VSME basic module datapoints an SME can realistically collect, without pretending to be full CSRD.
- Structures energy use and scope 1 and 2 emissions from invoices and meter data.
- Collects the workforce and governance datapoints in the format banks and large customers ask for.
- Turns the report into ready answers for the sustainability questionnaires large clients send down the chain.

## Use cases

- An SME asked by a large customer or bank for sustainability data
- A first voluntary sustainability report without hiring a consultant
- Answering a supplier questionnaire consistently instead of ad hoc
- Building a baseline to improve against next year

## Known limitations

The voluntary VSME standard, not full CSRD or ESRS reporting, and it is not an assured report. Emission factors must be taken from a current official source at the time of use. Output is a working draft for your own review.

## Example

**Request**

My biggest customer just sent me a 50-page ESG questionnaire. I am a 40-person manufacturing SME. How do I handle this without spending weeks on it?

**Result**

I will map your customer's request to the EU-endorsed VSME Basic module. This will provide a standardized report and a cover letter citing the value-chain cap to push back on excessive data requests. First, let's look at the questionnaire items related to energy and workforce.

## Questions

### How does this skill help SMEs that aren't yet required to meet full CSRD standards?

This skill streamlines the creation of a VSME-compliant voluntary report, focusing on energy usage, emissions, workforce data, and governance metrics specifically designed for SMEs facing pressure from banks or larger corporate clients.

### Do I need to be a sustainability expert to use the nex-vsme-sustainability-pack?

No specialized knowledge is required; the skill guides you through collecting specific datapoints from invoices, meter readings, and HR records to produce a structured, professional output.

### Can this skill help me respond to supplier sustainability questionnaires from my customers?

The report generates standardized responses that can be directly used to fill out the environmental and social questionnaires frequently sent by large corporate procurement departments.

### What exactly is included in the purchase, and are there any external requirements?

You will receive the AI agent skill configuration and a structured reporting template; however, you will need to provide your own current official emission factors to ensure the carbon calculations are accurate for your region.

### Is the final report generated by this skill ready for official audit or assurance?

This skill is designed to generate a 'working draft' for internal review and voluntary disclosure; it does not provide a third-party assured report or meet the full regulatory requirements of the ESRS for large enterprises.
