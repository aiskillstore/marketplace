## The problem

Cold outreach to Belgian and Dutch SMEs often fails because it leads with pricing or generic pitches. Developers and agencies struggle to find a proven Dutch-language hook that bypasses traditional gatekeepers without sounding like spam.

## What it does

- Generates a proven Dutch-language cold mail template optimized for the Belgian SME market.
- Implements the no-price, demo-first rule to increase initial response rates.
- Provides a structured website-audit hook combined with an AI-search angle.
- Includes mandatory opt-out lines and a single, clear call-to-action.

## Why this beats prompting it yourself

General LLM prompts often produce overly formal or translated-sounding Dutch text. This skill uses a specific outreach framework designed for the local market, ensuring the tone and structure match what actually gets replies from Belgian business owners.

## Use cases

- Generating a high-conversion opening mail for Dutch-speaking prospects.
- Testing a demo-first outreach strategy before committing to a full campaign.
- Quickly scaffolding a website-audit hook for technical sales.

## Known limitations

Provides only the initial opening template. Does not include follow-up sequences or objection handling playbooks.
