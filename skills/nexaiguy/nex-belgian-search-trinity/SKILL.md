## Dominate Belgian Search and AI Landscapes

In the modern search environment, ranking on Google (SEO) is no longer enough. To capture high-intent leads for Belgian SMEs, your content must also be optimized for AI Answer Engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity (AEO) and local "near me" intent (GEO). This skill automates the creation of high-converting, authority-building copy specifically tailored for the Belgian market.

## What it does

- Generates complete **/{service}-{gemeente}** location pages designed to dominate local search results.
- Drafts specialized **FAQ blocks** using proven patterns that encourage LLMs to cite your website verbatim.
- Provides accurate, up-to-date data on **Belgian subsidies (premies)** for Flanders, Brussels, and Wallonia to boost E-E-A-T.
- Constructs technically sound **Schema.org JSON-LD** (LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Breadcrumbs) to ensure search engines understand your entity data.
- Builds 6-month **content calendars** that balance local landing pages with high-value informational assets.
