# Nex Webshop Compliance EU

> Free skill by [Nex AI](https://nex-ai.be). This file is the public guide from [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/skills/nex-webshop-compliance-eu). Scripts, extension source, and templates from the original package are not in this folder.

## What it does

- Checks the pre-contract information duties and where each item must appear in the funnel.
- Verifies the order confirmation button wording and the no-hidden-cost rule at checkout.
- Builds the withdrawal form and the 14-day right notice, including the exceptions that actually apply.
- Reviews price display, delivery cost transparency and the cookie banner refusal path.

## Use cases

- Launching a webshop that will not get a consumer authority letter
- Auditing an existing shop before a marketing push
- Fixing a checkout that hides delivery costs until the last step
- Giving a client a concrete list of what to change

## Known limitations

EU and Belgian consumer law, not other markets, and member states add national requirements. Built for GDPR compliance rather than a guarantee of it. Output is a working audit for your own review, not legal advice.

**Request**

Audit my webshop at myshop.eu for EU compliance. We sell physical home decor and use a cookie banner.
