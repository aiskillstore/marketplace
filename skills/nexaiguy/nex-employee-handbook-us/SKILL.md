# Nex Employee Handbook US

> Free skill by [Nex AI](https://nex-ai.be). This file is the public guide from [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/skills/nex-employee-handbook-us). Scripts, extension source, and templates from the original package are not in this folder.

## The problem

Building a compliant employee handbook is a legal minefield for companies with remote or multi-state teams. Maintaining a single document that balances federal mandates, varying state sick leave laws, and strict NLRB conduct standards is nearly impossible without a dedicated legal team.

## What it does

- Drafts a "Core + Supplement" architecture to separate universal federal policies from high-variance state laws.
- Generates specific modules for EEO, ADA, PUMP Act, and FMLA based on company headcount thresholds.
- Performs a "Stericycle pass" to ensure conduct, social media, and confidentiality rules don't violate NLRA Section 7 rights.
- Identifies state-specific triggers for paid sick leave, pay transparency, and marijuana protections.
- Produces legally structured acknowledgment forms and annual review schedules for January compliance updates.

## Frameworks & tools

Compliant with 2026 US Federal standards, including NLRB Stericycle, FLSA PUMP Act, and EEOC PWFA guidelines.

## Why this beats prompting it yourself

Generic prompts often produce "hallucinated" state laws or outdated accrual rates. This skill enforces a specific structural logic that keeps at-will disclaimers defensible and ensures you don't miss the 75-mile radius rule for FMLA or state-level final paycheck timing.

## Use cases

- Creating an initial handbook for a startup making its first out-of-state hires.
- Reviewing an existing manual for compliance with the 2023 Stericycle conduct standards.
- Generating state-specific supplements for remote employees in high-regulation states like CA, NY, or IL.
- Drafting a PUMP Act compliant lactation policy for a growing office.

## Known limitations

Does not provide specific accrual rates or local city-level ordinance details. Requires final review by a licensed employment attorney in each relevant jurisdiction.
