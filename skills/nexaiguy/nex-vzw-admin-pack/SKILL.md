## Belgian Non-Profit Compliance & Administration

Running a Belgian non-profit (vzw/asbl) involves navigating the complex **Wetboek van vennootschappen en verenigingen (WVV)**. The Nex Vzw Admin Pack is a specialized tool for developers and advisors that automates the generation of legally compliant documentation, from initial bylaws to annual general assembly minutes.

## What it does

- **Bylaws Generation:** Creates "statuten skeletons" containing all mandatory clauses required by Art 2:9 WVV to avoid nullity.
- **Governance Support:** Generates meeting notices and minutes for General Assemblies (AV) and Boards of Directors, ensuring correct notice periods and voting quorums.
- **UBO Compliance:** Provides a structured workflow for UBO register filings, updates, and annual confirmations to avoid heavy administrative fines.
- **Filing Management:** Guides users through the flow for "griffie" and "Belgisch Staatsblad" filings (Forms I and II).
- **Accounting Assessment:** Automatically determines if a non-profit qualifies for simplified or double-entry bookkeeping based on the latest 2024/2026 financial thresholds.

## Why use this skill

Manual prompting often misses critical Belgian-specific legal nuances, such as the mandatory "disinterested purpose" vs. "activities" distinction or the specific 15-day notice periods for non-profits.
