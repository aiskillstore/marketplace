## Moderator card

### Protect

- Decision: [one sentence]
- Must-cover question: [one story anchor]
- Hypothesis to keep hidden: [one sentence]
- Time check: [minute]

### Say less

- Tell me about the last time…
- What happened next?
- How did you decide?
- What did you expect?
- Can you show me, if comfortable?
- Tell me more.

### Do not

- Pitch, teach, defend, or praise an answer.
- Ask every optional probe or fill every silence.
- Turn requests into votes or imagined intent into behavior.

If the conversation drifts: “I want to make sure I understand that specific situation. What happened next?”

Before ending, clarify contradictions, ask what was missed, confirm follow-up permission, and explain next steps without promising a product change.
