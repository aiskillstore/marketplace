## Scope-expansion rule

New ideas discovered during implementation remain **Options** until they pass the Commitment Boundary.

Do not implement adjacent scope merely because:

- it is technically easy;
- the agent already generated it;
- a competitor has it;
- it would make the product appear more complete;
- “we may need it later”.

## Allowed exceptions

Record only exceptions required for safety, correctness, compatibility, or completion of the committed value slice.
