# Lightweight PRFAQ

Use this template before an important Commitment decision. Keep it short enough to clarify value, not become a paperwork exercise.

## User

Who experiences the problem?

## Problem

What concrete problem exists today?

## Desired outcome

What changes for the user if this succeeds?

## Why now

Why is this worth attention now rather than later?

## Existing alternatives

How is the problem solved today, and why is that insufficient?

## Proposed capability

Describe the smallest meaningful capability in plain language. Avoid implementation details unless they are essential to value.

## FAQ

### What evidence supports this?


### What would make us defer it?


### What would make us discard it?


### What creates long-term ownership if we ship it?


## Non-goals

What is explicitly outside this commitment?
