# Pre-launch checklist

- Confirm the product's canonical HTTPS URL loads and represents the submitted product.
- Confirm the owner email is controlled by the maker and suitable for verification messages.
- Check the category, alternatives, live launch calendar, and current free-slots counter.
- Keep the tagline specific and factual. Avoid unverifiable superlatives and unsupported claims.
- Describe who it serves, what it does, and any relevant pricing using maker-confirmed details.
- Confirm the final name, URL, email, tagline, description, and public handle with the maker before submission.
- Explain that the owner must complete one of the returned verification methods.
