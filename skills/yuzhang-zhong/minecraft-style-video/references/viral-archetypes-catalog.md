# Viral Archetypes Catalog / 六大高赞 Minecraft 短视频剧场模板

Use these six proven 9:16 vertical storyboard archetypes when the user wants a ready-to-shoot Minecraft short covering characters, mobs, and voxel objects.

## 1. `diamond-creeper-ambush` · 《挖到钻石秒遇苦力怕》 (Cave Mining Comedy Hook)

- **Featured Cast & Objects**: `steve-miner` + `creeper-mob` + `diamond-ore` (`16×16×16`) + `diamond-pickaxe` (`16×16×1`)
- **Recommended Shader**: `rtx-emissive` or `bsl-sunlight`
- **4-Shot Breakdown**:
  1. **Hook (0–3.8s)**: Steve swings his stepped `16×16×1` Diamond Pickaxe into a glowing cyan `16×16×16` Diamond Ore block in a torch-lit cave (`"Diamonds?!"` / `"出钻石了?!"`).
  2. **Escalation 1 (3.8–7.5s)**: The ore shatters into square pixel cubes and a spinning miniature diamond item, revealing a green armless **Creeper** standing right behind the gap (`"Tssss..."` / `"嘶——"`).
  3. **Escalation 2 (7.5–11.3s)**: The Creeper flashes solid white and swells in boxy scale while Steve's `8×8` pixel eyes widen into hollow squares and his stiff `4×12×4` arms fly up (`"RUN!!"` / `"快跑!!"`).
  4. **Payoff & Loop (11.3–15.0s)**: White cubic explosion smoke blocks burst outward; Steve tips sideways in a classic red-tinted hurt pose beside a dropped pickaxe (`"Respawn..."` / `"重生点见..."`).
