# AI Detector

Detect the user-provided content for AI-generated patterns and assess the likelihood of AI generation. Generate an evidence-based AI detection report in HTML format. The report must include a percentage-based circular indicator card that visually represents the AI likelihood score using the ring's fill level and an appropriate color. It must also include a download link for the analyzed content.
