# Voice profile template

Use this optional file to preserve a named writer or brand voice. Keep it short and specific.

## Core voice

- Tone: direct, warm, formal, conversational, or another clear description.
- Sentence rhythm: short, varied, technical, literary, or another useful pattern.
- Level of polish: casual, professional, editorial, or publication-ready.

## Non-negotiables

- Punctuation rules, including whether long dashes are allowed.
- Approved spelling, capitalization, and product names.
- Terms to avoid in public copy.
- Required accessibility, legal, or brand language.

## Editing boundary

- Whether the editor may correct verified facts.
- Whether it may reformat headings, lists, and quotations.
- Any audience or channel rules that change the editing depth.
