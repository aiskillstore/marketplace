# English writing: audience, precision, voice

Use with the genre module. Derive cadence and formality from the deliverable and actual voice samples. A01–A09 and A13–A17 supply editorial heuristics and tool patterns, not authorship evidence or universal performance.

## Establish variety and relationship

Keep the requested English variety, spelling convention, terminology and citation style consistent. If unspecified, follow the supplied draft or destination. Do not mistake a non-native writer's direct vocabulary or regular syntax for defective writing.

Separate register from personality. Academic English may need measured hedging; support messages need usable actions; a sales page needs a grounded offer. Contractions, first person, fragments, humor and informal idiom are contextual choices, not compulsory signs of humanity.

## Make sentences do work

- Prefer a precise common verb when it carries the meaning. Retain a term of art when the common substitute changes it.
- Make subject/action relationships easy to follow. Use passive voice when the agent is unknown, irrelevant, sensitive or conventionally backgrounded. Never invent an actor to force active voice.
- Remove empty intensifiers and framing. Preserve adverbs defining frequency, uncertainty, scope, sequence or measurement.
- Preserve logic. “Although,” “because,” “unless,” “only,” “may” and “not” can change a claim.
- Keep entity labels stable. Do not cycle through “platform,” “solution,” “ecosystem” and “tool” for one entity when the distinction matters.
- Split overload where helpful; retain a longer sentence if it clearly carries a relationship. Use no mandatory sentence-length band.

## Diagnose familiar patterns by function

Review clusters such as “in today's rapidly evolving landscape,” “delve,” “unlock,” “seamless,” “robust” and “game-changing.” A paper may legitimately discuss robustness; the problem is unsupported or generic use, not the word itself.

Replace stock contrast reveals, self-answered questions, repeated triads, faux profundity and mic-drop endings when they substitute for argument. Preserve a real contrast, an actual three-part result, a useful FAQ or deliberate literary form.

Use em dashes, semicolons and parentheses where they help the relationship and fit the writer. Their presence or absence is not an authorship test. Replacing every mark with a comma can create run-ons.

Avoid synonym laundering. Replacing “leverage” with “harness” does not repair an empty claim. State the mechanism, evidence, result or limitation; identify missing information rather than inventing detail.

## Preserve voice and cultural context

Extract stance, vocabulary, directness, humor, rhythm and formality from a legitimate voice sample. Do not import its facts, identity or experiences into a new piece.

Keep culturally specific places, food, relationships and social expectations that belong to the author. Do not replace them with globally familiar references because those are more common in model output. A language edit should not erase identity or turn every non-native voice into corporate English.

When translating Persian, rebuild natural English order without adding cause, certainty, qualifications or personal agency. For market adaptation, distinguish changed presentation from a changed fact or commercial promise.

## Original calibration examples

### Product

Given: a tool merges CSV files; no speed test exists.

Weak: “Unlock seamless productivity with our revolutionary data ecosystem.”

Useful: “Merge your CSV files into one table.”

Unsupported: “Finish weekly reporting ten times faster.” Specific wording still needs evidence.

### Scientific qualification

Given: “The treatment was associated with a small reduction in this sample.”

Faithful: “In this sample, the treatment was associated with a small reduction.”

Reject: “The treatment reduced the outcome.” The edit removes association language and scope.

### Authorial rhythm

Given: “I like the idea. I still don't trust the numbers.”

Keep it when it fits the work. Do not inflate it to a sentence about “considerable promise” and “underlying quantitative frameworks” just to sound professional.

## Final review

Check agreement, pronoun reference, tense, articles, parallelism, quotation fidelity, spelling variety, units and citations. Read the lead and ending for reader value. Review rhythm across the piece without a numerical quota. Compare the final text with its evidence and voice contract.
