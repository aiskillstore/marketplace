# Quality gates and evidence-first review

Use this module before final delivery, or when asked to review or improve a draft. Scale the work to the assignment: a short message needs a short check; a research document needs claim-level review. Load [creative-and-editorial.md](creative-and-editorial.md) for creative work, voice-sensitive editing, or a batch of related texts.

## Evidence boundary

- **R01:** In controlled English answer comparisons, separating evaluation dimensions exposed factuality problems hidden by holistic preferences; evaluator biases remained.
- **R02:** Feedback on short, artificially perturbed English stories could be specific and plausible while missing the main defect. Directly asking for one problem was not the strongest tested prompt.
- **R05:** A petition-platform natural experiment reported changed language and higher text ratings without improved measured engagement outcomes. Its proposed explanations were hypotheses.

The procedures below are original editorial design informed by these findings, not a reproduced intervention or a validated improvement guarantee. English results do not establish Persian effect sizes. Source details and access limits belong in the evidence register; normal writing does not require network access or paid services.

## XQ-01: Evaluate dimensions separately

Record a brief verdict for each relevant dimension: **pass**, **revise**, **unverified**, or **not applicable**. A verdict needs a concrete reason. Keep this working review internal unless the user asks for it or an unresolved issue changes how the deliverable can be used.

| Dimension | Inspect | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Claim integrity | Names, numbers, attribution, quotations, uncertainty, scope | Unsupported assertion, altered qualifier, invented source or experience |
| Task fit | Requested audience, purpose, format, constraints and edit depth | Main question unanswered or requested result missing |
| Reasoning and structure | Claim–support links, chronology, definitions, referents | Contradiction, missing causal step, unexplained transition |
| Voice and cultural fit | Author stance, register, local details, relationship to reader | Unrequested stance change, stereotyping, erased identity-bearing detail |
| Language and usability | Readability, terminology, grammar, script and direction | Wording blocks understanding or format obstructs use |
| Intended outcome | Observable success criterion, if one exists | A style score is presented as proof of sales, learning or persuasion |

Do not average these verdicts into one quality number. A serious failure in claim integrity or task fit blocks a ready verdict even when the prose is fluent. An unverified material claim must be verified, qualified, removed if dispensable, or surfaced clearly for the user's decision. Do not label it correct because two model passes agree.

**Exception:** Pure proofreading preserves the author's assertions and intended structure. Flag a consequential issue separately rather than silently expanding the commission into fact-checking or rewriting.

## XQ-02: Establish evidence before polishing

Before a substantive rewrite, preserve a small invariant list: the central claim, necessary evidence, names, quantities, dates, citations, technical terms, qualifications, author stance, and any explicit constraints. For translation, add the relationship between claims and their supporting citations.

Check consequential claims against supplied material or an appropriate accessible primary source. A matching topic or confident wording is not support. Distinguish the source's finding, the author's interpretation, and your proposed inference. Label invented illustrations as hypothetical; never turn them into testimony, a case study, or measured results.

After revision, compare the invariant list with the actual revised text. Check that “may,” “in this sample,” “up to,” a null result, a condition, or a counterexample did not disappear. A shorter sentence is useful only if it still says the right thing.

**Exception:** If sources are unavailable or outside the authorized scope, retain a truthful uncertainty note and deliver the useful remainder. The writing skill itself is not evidence for an external factual claim.

## XQ-03: Diagnose, prioritize, repair, stop

1. Read for the assignment's criteria before editing individual words. Identify supported defects across relevant categories; do not assume every draft contains a hidden fatal flaw.
2. Rank by consequence: a false or missing claim, broken logic or chronology, audience mismatch, then local wording. A short message can make tone the main issue; a story can make missing motivation the main issue.
3. For the leading issue, identify the exact passage, explain its effect on the reader, and make the smallest repair that resolves it. For a review, give a concrete next action and preserve the author's choice among valid solutions.
4. Re-read the repaired passage in context. Verify that the repair actually resolves the diagnosed issue and preserves the invariants. Then address any remaining material defect.
5. Finish when the required content works, material failures are resolved or disclosed, and another pass offers only interchangeable preferences. Do not manufacture criticisms, inflate praise, or keep rewriting to satisfy a ritual number of rounds.

A concise final review can name the main issue and the next useful edit. Do not force all feedback into one sentence: a brief taxonomy-backed review may need several points before an honest priority emerges. Praise only a specific strength that is present; do not use reassurance to obscure an unresolved defect.

**Exception:** Intentional ambiguity, unusual chronology, repetition, fragments, and unreliable narration can be artistic choices. Test their function against the brief and the surrounding work before treating them as mistakes.

## XQ-04: Keep outcomes distinct from ratings

For copy, advocacy, instruction or product writing, identify what the reader should understand or do and what would count as success. When available, use an appropriate outcome measure such as correct task completion, qualified enquiries, or a defined conversion event. State whether evidence is observed, experimental, merely anticipated, or absent.

Describe a rewrite's visible change accurately: “The request is now explicit” or “The prerequisite appears before the steps.” Do not claim “more persuasive,” “higher converting,” “better learning,” or a percentage improvement as an established result without relevant measurement. An editor's preference, readability formula, detector score and actual outcome are different observations.

**Exception:** A user can request a creative pitch or persuasive tone without an experiment. Produce the writing and describe its intended effect as a goal, not a measured result. Do not initiate external tests or contact readers without authorization.

## Original examples: inspect the claim and the main defect

These examples are invented teaching material, not research data or accounts of actual users.

**English, preserving a scientific limit**

Source statement: “In this small sample, the revised instructions may have reduced setup errors.”

Meaning-changing polish: “The revised instructions reduce setup errors.”

Faithful revision: “The revised instructions may have reduced setup errors in this small sample.”

The main defect is the lost evidential limit, not the repeated word “instructions.”

**Persian, restoring a missing referent without inventing a fact**

Draft: «لیلا کلید را به نگهبان داد. او قفل را باز کرد.»

Review: «در جملهٔ دوم روشن نیست چه کسی قفل را باز می‌کند. اگر منظورتان نگهبان است، نام او را تکرار کنید.»

Conditional revision: «لیلا کلید را به نگهبان داد. نگهبان قفل را باز کرد.»

Do not adopt the conditional revision as fact until context or the author resolves the referent.

**Persian, preserving uncertainty**

Draft: «در این نمونهٔ کوچک، شاید راهنمای تازه خطاهای راه‌اندازی را کمتر کرده باشد.»

Faithful revision: «در این نمونهٔ کوچک، راهنمای تازه ممکن است خطاهای راه‌اندازی را کاهش داده باشد.»

Both retain the limited sample and uncertainty. Choose the version that fits the document's register; neither establishes causation.

## Release check

Read the delivered text, not only the checklist. Confirm that each material change has a reason, unsupported additions are absent or explicitly hypothetical, the main defect is actually repaired, and unresolved limits are easy to find. Preserve a successful original sentence when changing it serves no purpose.
