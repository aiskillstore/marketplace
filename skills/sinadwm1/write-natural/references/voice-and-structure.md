# Preserve voice while improving structure

Use alongside [Anti-slop](anti-slop.md). Voice includes stance, degree of formality, syntax, rhythm, culturally meaningful politeness, recurring vocabulary, and what the author chooses to leave unresolved. Natural revision preserves those functions while making the intended meaning easier to follow. These rules are editorial synthesis, not a detector or an empirically validated Persian style model.

## VS01 — Choose the destination's register

Infer the register from the requested deliverable and its reader. A casual request for a proposal can require formal prose. Different deliverables in one request may need different registers. Follow explicit user direction; ask a compact question only if ambiguity would materially change the work. Otherwise use a stated, modest assumption and proceed. Never infer informality solely from “humanize.” [A06, A08–A12]

| Destination | English starting point | Persian starting point | What may legitimately remain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research or scholarly paraphrase | Precise, qualified, discipline appropriate | علمی؛ صورت‌های کامل فعل و اصطلاحات ثابت | Cautious findings, passive constructions, standard sections and citations |
| Proposal, report, customer correspondence | Clear professional language | رسمی و روان؛ معمولاً «شما» و فعل نوشتاری | Necessary politeness, obligations, qualifications and formal vocabulary |
| Administrative letter | Required institutional conventions | اداری؛ عنوان و سمت گیرنده و احترام متناسب | Salutation, respectful request and closing formulas |
| Blog or explanatory newsletter | Accessible language appropriate to readers' expertise | معمولاً نیمه‌رسمی با فعل نوشتاری | A useful aside, clear stance and necessary definitions |
| Personal message or established social voice | Match the actual relationship and sample | محاوره‌نویسی در صورت تناسب با مخاطب | Contractions, particles, fragments and humor that belong to the author |
| Fiction, memoir, poetry | Follow intended literary effect | زبان ادبی یا گفت‌وگو مطابق اثر | Ambiguity, repetition, imagery, paced revelation and purposeful deviation |

These are defaults, not fixed platform laws. A technical Telegram channel may use formal Persian; an internal email may be familiar. A company can require a recurring three-part status template. Match its actual brief rather than imposing an invented universal voice. [A10, A11, A12]

## VS02 — Use real evidence of voice

Read a relevant sample when available. Prefer the same language, genre, audience, and stage of polish. Note a few concrete traits: directness, clause length, familiar terms, punctuation, stance, humor, transitions, and politeness. Preserve useful traits without amplifying every quirk. One short message does not establish a complete style. A word-exception profile cannot represent cadence or viewpoint. Without a sample, promise an appropriate editorial register, not a perfect match to the person. [A02, A06, A08, A09]

Do not invent lived experience, private feelings, confidence, irritation, admissions, credentials, or jokes to make the text personal. Recast only what the author supplied. Fiction permits invented detail within the creative brief; it does not turn a real person's biography or testimonial into fiction. [A02, A08]

Original examples:

- EN source: “I like the course, but I'm still unsure about the final project.” Safe: “I like the course. I'm still unsure about the final project.” Unsafe: “The course finally gave me confidence.”
- FA source: «از دوره راضی‌ام، ولی هنوز دربارهٔ پروژهٔ پایانی مطمئن نیستم.» Safe: «از دوره راضی‌ام. هنوز دربارهٔ پروژهٔ پایانی مطمئن نیستم.» Unsafe: «این دوره بالاخره به من اعتمادبه‌نفس داد.»

## VS03 — Protect the author's choices

Before editing, mark approved copy, exact quotations, titles, formal names, identifiers, citations, figures, and intentional placeholders. Leave approved or locked passages intact unless the requested scope includes them. Do not “improve” marketing copy incidentally while implementing a different task. Strong sentences can stay verbatim; consistency of polish is not a reason to erase character. [A02, A06, A08, A09, A25]

Keep a small meaning inventory for consequential edits:

- Who acts, speaks, believes, promises, or observes?
- What happened, is intended, is possible, or is predicted?
- Which quantity belongs to which item, unit, date, baseline, or population?
- What limits the claim: only, most, partly, may, at least, up to, not measured?
- What emotion or evaluation does the author own?

Preserve both strong and cautious assertions. An unknown date must not become a claim that nobody has documented it. A risk must not become an event. No statistically significant difference does not establish equality. A supported cultural detail must not disappear into generic praise. [A01, A08–A11]

## VS04 — Carry the logic across the edit

Track cause, contrast, concession, condition, sequence, simultaneity, comparison, and reference. Keep a connector when it states a real relationship or carries the writer's stance. Delete a habitual transition only if the same relation remains recoverable without added work. Do not introduce causality to smooth two adjacent facts. If the relation is ambiguous, retain the wording or flag the question. [A07, A08, A09, A18]

Original examples:

- EN: “We postponed the visit because the road was closed.” Keep the causal relationship. “We postponed the visit. The road was closed.” weakens the explicit connection.
- FA: «چون جاده بسته بود، بازدید را عقب انداختیم.» حفظ «چون» رابطه را روشن نگه می‌دارد؛ دو جملهٔ جدا لزوماً همان معنا را منتقل نمی‌کنند.
- EN: “The two checks run at the same time” must not become “The checks run in order.”
- FA: «دو بررسی هم‌زمان اجرا می‌شوند» با «بررسی‌ها به‌ترتیب اجرا می‌شوند» هم‌معنا نیست.

An aside such as “unfortunately” / «متأسفانه» may express an actual stance. Its lack of a new factual proposition does not make it expendable.

## VS05 — Build a progression the task needs

For an argument, connect the question, evidence, interpretation, and consequence. For an explanation, introduce each prerequisite before using it. For a procedure, give prerequisites, ordered actions, expected results, and relevant limits. For a narrative, preserve the intended sequence and revelation. For a comparison, use the same criteria for each option. For reference entries or FAQs, optimize independent lookup. [A07, A08, A10, A12, A21]

Reverse-outline a long draft with one short description of each paragraph's job. Merge duplicate jobs, move a prerequisite before its use, or restore a missing link from supplied material. A paragraph need not start with a thesis sentence, and an ending need not repeat it. A literature review may need synthesis across sources; an annotated bibliography legitimately discusses them separately. A fixed report or journal structure is not a defect. [A06–A12]

Test order only where order matters. If several argumentative paragraphs can be shuffled without changing anything, determine whether they need a stronger progression. Do not apply that test as a failure criterion to inventories, independent findings, dictionaries, or modular documentation.

## VS06 — Let cadence follow the thought

Read the text as an intended reader would: spoken language for dialogue or a personal message, careful written language for a paper or legal document. Repair a genuine stumble, unclear antecedent, overloaded clause, abrupt handoff, or accidental repeated opening. Keep a long clear sentence, a purposeful fragment, a parallel comparison, or repeated phrasing that serves the voice. [A02, A06–A09]

Do not target a coefficient of variation, force a short sentence after a long one, mandate a fragment per paragraph, add errors, or alter sentence lengths randomly. Those operations can make prose more mechanical. No numerical cadence target establishes naturalness in either language. Tone and complexity must fit the reader; expert vocabulary is useful when expert readers need it. [A03, A08, A09, A20, A21]

Original examples:

- EN: “The editor checked the title. The editor checked the date. The editor sent the proof.” → “The editor checked the title and date, then sent the proof.” Keep the repeated opener instead if a deliberate rhetorical effect requires it.
- FA: «ویراستار عنوان را بررسی کرد. ویراستار تاریخ را بررسی کرد. ویراستار نمونه را فرستاد.» → «ویراستار عنوان و تاریخ را بررسی کرد، سپس نمونه را فرستاد.»

## VS07 — Preserve linguistic and cultural specificity

For Persian, keep the distinction between formal written forms and colloquial written speech. Verb endings can carry the subject without repeated «شما». Choose «تو» or «شما» from the actual relationship, not a desire to sound intimate. Preserve appropriate تعارف, honorifics, familiar loanwords, local references, and jokes; reduce ceremony only when it obstructs the task. An Iranian default is not automatically correct for every Persian-speaking community. [A10, A11]

For English, preserve the author's regional spelling and valid dialect unless the brief requests a house style. Do not use a historical manual to replace contemporary singular “they,” erase dialect, or impose obsolete usage. Review inclusive-language suggestions in context; a tool cannot determine an identified person's pronouns or a community's preferred terminology. Quotations remain exact. [A07, A16]

Original examples:

- Formal FA: «لطفاً نسخهٔ اصلاح‌شده را ارسال کنید.»
- Familiar FA, if the relationship supports it: «لطفاً نسخهٔ اصلاح‌شده رو بفرست.»
- Keep an appropriate «با سلام و احترام» in an administrative letter; do not replace it with a casual greeting solely to remove a formula.
- When the brief supplies a price in تومان, retain that unit. Do not substitute ریال, dollars, a Western retailer, or an invented local example to make the prose sound familiar.

Orthography and layout are separate review layers. Preserve Persian joining and نیم‌فاصله; normalize confirmed keyboard errors only in editable Persian prose. Keep Latin code, names, notation, links, and version strings exact. Confirm mixed-direction rendering in the actual output format when layout is part of the task. This module supplies no universal claim about a particular renderer's RTL behavior. [A09, A10]

## VS08 — Keep content quality distinct from genre taste

Assess usefulness, relevance, factual support, appropriate perspective, coherence, natural phrasing, repetition, and effort of reading separately. A polished sentence can still lack a point. A formal sentence can be exactly right. A quiet factual passage need not acquire a hot take; an opinion piece should retain the author's supported stance rather than being neutralized. [A06, A08, A09, A20, A21]

If adapting to another genre, change selection and order to suit that reader while preserving the brief's required substance. Keep a consequential limitation beside the claim or action it qualifies. Do not turn educational writing into an advertisement midway through, or convert a feature into an unverified benefit. Platform word counts, engagement tactics, ranking promises, and fixed rhetorical budgets require separate evidence or an explicit brief. [A10, A12, A21]

## VS09 — Finish with four review passes

Scale the review to the task; a short sentence does not need an elaborate ceremony. For a substantial edit, make four distinct passes. These are responsibilities, not claims that four agents or independent people reviewed the work. [A02, A06, A08, A09]

1. **Purpose and voice:** read the full available input; identify the reader, register, core point, protected content, and recognizable voice traits. Note missing context that would change the edit.
2. **Substance and structure:** make the smallest sufficient intervention. Classify spans as keep, repair, cut, or reorganize. Repair relations and progression using supplied material; flag missing substance.
3. **Language and flow:** correct actual usage problems, awkward phrasing, and presentation. Read the result in its intended register. Check Persian formality, joining, punctuation, and mixed-language segments separately from English mechanics.
4. **Meaning and final fit:** compare the exact final text with the input for assertions, attribution, quantities, negation, scope, relationships, voice, and protected content. Check that the opening, ending, and format fulfill the request. Recheck any final repair.

Stop when the remaining differences are preferences, or when a correction would require guessing. Return the requested final text or format; add a short note only for material unresolved facts, ambiguities, or requested change explanations. In audit mode, give a locator, the specific defect, its severity, and a proposed direction; leave the draft unchanged. Do not expose internal checklists in a standalone article or email unless requested.

## VS10 — Explain the limits honestly

Call findings editorial observations. Do not assert authorship from style, certify “undetectability,” claim a detector was passed without running it, or claim improvement was independently measured when it was self-reviewed. Simulated audience reactions, if requested, are hypotheses rather than real reader feedback. External tools are optional; missing tools do not prevent a direct, careful edit. [A01, A06, A08, A09, A13–A17, A20–A24]

The source register records where each adopted, adapted, contextual, or rejected idea came from. Source authors do not endorse this synthesis. The final authority for an editorial preference is the task, audience, and user's chosen style, subject to accurate representation of the underlying material.
