# Examples / مثال‌ها

These examples are hypothetical instructions, not customer results or detector experiments.

## Academic fidelity / حفظ ادعای علمی

Input: “In this small sample, the intervention may have reduced errors.”

Request: “Use $write-natural to improve flow. Preserve sample scope and uncertainty.”

Acceptable: “The intervention may have reduced errors in this small sample.”

Reject: “The intervention reduces errors.” That removes both limitations.

فارسی: «در این نمونهٔ کوچک، مداخله ممکن است خطاها را کاهش داده باشد.» نباید به «مداخله خطاها را کاهش می‌دهد» تبدیل شود.

## Product copy / معرفی محصول

Supplied fictional facts: three USB ports; two-year warranty. No measured speed or competitor comparison supplied.

Request: “Use $write-natural to describe this product for a first-time buyer. Use only these facts. No testimonials or superiority claims.”

A bounded description may name the ports and warranty. It cannot add “fastest”, “best-selling”, or a fabricated customer quote.

فارسی: «با $write-natural فقط بر اساس سه درگاه USB و ضمانت دوساله معرفی بنویس. مقایسهٔ سرعت، میزان فروش یا رضایت مشتری نساز.»

## Bounded edit / ویرایش محدود

“Use $write-natural to change only the title. Keep the body byte-for-byte unchanged.”

«با $write-natural فقط عنوان را عوض کن؛ بدنه بدون تغییر بماند.»

## Critique / نقد

“Use $write-natural to critique this draft. Quote the affected passage, explain its reader impact and suggest a repair. Do not rewrite the entire text.”

«ایراد واقعی را با عبارت دقیق نشان بده؛ برای پر کردن فهرست، ایراد سلیقه‌ای نساز.»

## Detector-aware / بازبینی با توجه به تشخیص

“Use $write-natural to revise formulaic phrasing while preserving voice and evidence. No detector has been run; do not report a passed scan.”

«متن را طبیعی‌تر کن، اما تجربهٔ شخصی، غلط عمدی یا ادعای عبور از آشکارساز اضافه نکن.»

## Voice and translation / لحن و ترجمه

Provide a real sample from the same genre and audience. Ask the assistant to preserve directness, formality and terminology. Do not expect a short chat message to establish a complete personal style.
