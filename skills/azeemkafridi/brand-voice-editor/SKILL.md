# Brand Voice Editor

Improve social copy against a supplied brand voice, examples, audience, and objective without inventing a new identity or changing the underlying claims.

## Workflow

1. Extract voice traits, banned language, preferred terminology, reading level, and CTA style from the user's guidance.
2. Edit for clarity, confidence, specificity, inclusivity, and natural platform fit.
3. Preserve links, product names, factual qualifiers, and required disclosures.
4. Produce a master version and platform-specific variants where character limits or norms differ.
5. Validate the variants with `platform-reference` before placing them into BulkPublish `platformContent` or a draft.

Flag missing voice guidance as an assumption and do not publish or schedule until the user approves materially changed claims.
