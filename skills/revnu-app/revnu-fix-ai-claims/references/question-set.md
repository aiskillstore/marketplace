# Brand questions for collecting answers

Use these only when no `revnu-geo-check` report or pasted answers exist. They
ask about the business by name, because this skill checks what engines
believe about it, not whether they recommend it (that's `revnu-geo-check`).

Replace `<Name>`, `<category>`, `<competitor>` and `<use case>` from
`.agents/revnu-growth.md` or the site. Ask each question in a fresh session,
twice per engine, and keep the cited links.

## Identity
1. What is <Name>?
2. Who makes <Name>, and where is the company based?
3. Is <Name> the same as <namesake>? (Only if a namesake is known.)

## Offer and price
4. How much does <Name> cost? Is there a free plan or trial?
5. What are <Name>'s plans and what does each include?

## Capability
6. Does <Name> integrate with <the integration buyers ask about most>?
7. What are <Name>'s main limitations?

## Fit and comparison
8. Is <Name> good for <use case>?
9. <Name> vs <competitor>: which should I pick?
10. What are the best alternatives to <Name>?

## Status (after a change)
11. Is <Name> still available? / What is <Name> called now? (After a rebrand.)
12. What changed in <Name>'s pricing in <year>? (After a price change.)

## Labels to record with every answer

- Engine and surface: `ChatGPT app (search on)`, `OpenAI API with web search
  tool`, `Perplexity API`, `Gemini API with Google Search grounding`, `host web
  search (no engine)`, `user screenshot`.
- Date, question, run number, cited URLs.
- Whether the answer browsed (cited URLs present) or answered from memory.
  A claim from memory is fixed slowly, through the pages future training and
  retrieval will read; a claim with a citation points straight at its source.
