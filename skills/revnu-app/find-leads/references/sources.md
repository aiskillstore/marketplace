# Sources

Platform and legal rules this skill relies on. All pages were read on
2026-11-05; each rule is stated "as of 2026-11-05" and can change.

| Rule used in the skill | Source | As of |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn members agree not to use software, scripts, robots or crawlers to scrape or copy the services, including profiles, and not to use bots or unauthorized automated methods to access the services or add or download contacts (User Agreement section 8.2, items 2 and 13; item 3 covers bypassing access controls and use limits). | LinkedIn User Agreement: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement | 2026-11-05 |

## This skill's own defaults (not platform rules)

The 10-25 list size, 90-day freshness window, "2-3 times more candidates than
the target", and ranking by freshness and directness of the signal are
judgement calls in this skill, not published rules.

## Not verified here

- Other sites' terms (job boards, review sites, directories) vary and were not
  read for this draft. The skill tells the agent to read only public pages and
  respect each site's limits rather than asserting any site's terms.
- Country-specific rules on prospecting data (GDPR legitimate-interest
  assessments, Germany, Canada) were not checked against primary sources;
  the skill does not state them.
