# Evidence limitations

- Resolve entity and recipient ambiguity before aggregation.
- Distinguish prime awards, subawards, obligations, deobligations, current values, potential ceilings, and transactions.
- Mark partial fiscal years and incomplete lookback periods.
- Label top-N, value-sorted, keyword, description, and capped samples.
- Record missing attachments, null competition data, incomplete subaward coverage, and inaccessible sources.
- An absence of public records is not proof that a capability, relationship, action, or risk does not exist.
- SAM registration is not proof of responsibility or performance capability.
- Historical awards do not guarantee future budget, recompete, or opportunity timing.
- CALC+ ceiling rates are not paid-rate or price-reasonableness evidence.
- Company websites and press releases are self-published claims.

Record exact queries, retrieval times, counts, transformations, exclusions, and uncertainty. Label model reasoning as inference.
