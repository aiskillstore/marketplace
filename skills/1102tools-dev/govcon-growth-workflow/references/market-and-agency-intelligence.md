# Market and agency intelligence

Define the business question, customer level, NAICS/PSC, offerings, geography, date range, and comparison set. Keep government-wide, department, agency, and sub-tier scopes separate.

Analyze spending, award counts, recipients, vehicles, competition fields, set-asides, transaction trends, forecasts, notices, and official strategy documents where available. Deduplicate recipient aliases and preserve negative obligations. Mark partial periods and missing fields.

Distinguish market size from addressable market, historical demand from future budget, and agency statements from funded opportunities. Do not call a market attractive without applying the user's capabilities, access, strategy, and economics.
