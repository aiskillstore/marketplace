# Competitor and incumbent intelligence

Resolve the exact entity before aggregation. Prefer UEI, CAGE, recipient ID, legal name, and known aliases. Keep unresolved records separate.

Analyze public prime awards, transactions, subawards, agencies, vehicles, NAICS/PSC, geography, apparent offerings, partners, and recent changes. Distinguish obligations from ceiling values and current award amounts. State subaward and description coverage limits.

Do not infer performance quality, future intent, responsibility, proprietary capability, margin, financial health, customer relationship, or staffing from award data alone. Treat company websites and press releases as self-published claims unless independently supported.
