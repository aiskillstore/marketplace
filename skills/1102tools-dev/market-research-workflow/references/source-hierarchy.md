# Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order when they address the same proposition:

1. Supplied approved acquisition records for the user's own requirement, subject to status and precedence confirmation.
2. Current statute, FAR, agency supplement, binding policy, and official acquisition guidance.
3. Official federal data reached through the installed MCP servers.
4. Official agency, contract vehicle, program, standards, and public catalog pages.
5. Primary commercial sources such as manufacturer, publisher, vendor, distributor, and public price lists.
6. Credible secondary sources used only with attribution and cross-checking.
7. Model inference, explicitly labeled and never substituted for missing evidence.

Record retrieval date, relevant scope, and limitations. Prefer multiple independent sources for a consequential finding. A federal award history does not by itself establish commercial availability, current vendor capability, responsibility, price reasonableness, or the Rule of Two.
