# Demo photograph credits

These are existing photographs distributed with scikit-image, not Karl's photography, AI-generated pictures, customer work or endorsements. They are used to make a reproducible color-grading demonstration. No person, agency, venue or company depicted endorses Yingse.

| Bundled file | Original dataset item | Credit / documented status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `inputs/portrait.png` | `astronaut.png` | NASA photograph of astronaut Eileen Collins; scikit-image documents it as public domain, with no known copyright restrictions. |
| `inputs/still-life.png` | `coffee.png` | Photograph by Rachel Michetti, courtesy of Pikolo Espresso Bar; scikit-image documents the photographer's CC0 dedication. |
| `inputs/twilight.jpg` | `rocket.jpg` | SpaceX photograph of the Falcon 9 / DSCOVR launch; scikit-image documents it as released into the public domain. |

Source and rights descriptions were checked on 2026-11-13:

- [Astronaut dataset documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut)
- [Coffee dataset documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.coffee)
- [Rocket dataset documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.rocket)

`source-hashes.json` records the exact bundled input bytes. Inputs are copied from the installed scikit-image dataset without retouching. Example outputs and gallery panels are derived by applying the bundled creative LUTs; gallery panels are resized and labelled for display. These examples illustrate specific images, not universal suitability or objective improvement.
