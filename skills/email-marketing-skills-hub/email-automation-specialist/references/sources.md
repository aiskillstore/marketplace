# Public source register

Checked 2026-11-04. Summaries in this package are original and intentionally bounded. These are primary public sources, not guarantees that every account has a feature or that a campaign is lawful. Revalidate legal, provider, product and API details before execution. Never infer access or permission from a documentation page.

- FTC, **CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business**: US commercial email obligations and transactional/commercial distinctions. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- ICO, **Marketing and data protection in detail**: UK electronic marketing, recipient-type distinctions and opt-outs. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/direct-marketing-and-data-protection/marketing-and-data-protection-in-detail/
- Google, **Email sender guidelines**: personal Gmail sender authentication, infrastructure and unsubscribe requirements. https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en
- Google, **Email sender guidelines FAQ**: bulk-sender interpretation and one-click unsubscribe clarification. https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414?hl=en
- Apple, **Mail Privacy Protection & Privacy**: remote-content prefetching and limits on inferred email activity. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/mail-privacy-protection/
- Poptin, **Autoresponder**: publicly described triggers, templates and scheduling. https://www.poptin.com/features/autoresponder/
- Poptin, **Integrations**: publicly listed CRM/email connections. https://www.poptin.com/integrations/
- Poptin Help Center, **How can I get the leads from my poptins?**: lead retrieval and integration entry point. https://help.poptin.com/en/article/how-can-i-get-the-leads-from-my-poptins-14943rs/

Journey architecture, idempotency, launch controls and test designs in this package are recommended engineering practices, not claims that these sources prescribe a specific implementation. Timing examples must be adapted rather than treated as research-proven best times.
