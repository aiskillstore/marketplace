# Public popup benchmarks

Use for benchmark questions and popup-test planning. Source: [Poptin’s 2026 report](https://www.poptin.com/blog/popup-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026/), published October 8; reviewed November 4, 2026.

January–September retained-cohort submission median: **1.30%**; upper-quartile threshold: **2.67%**; upper-decile threshold: **5.29%** (2,305 popups). Tracked-click median: **2.95%** (3,580 popups). Percentiles are thresholds, not averages of top performers.

These vendor observations are not universal ecommerce targets. Eligibility required 1,000 views and 14 exposure days; the lowest global decile was excluded separately by objective. Repeated views/events are possible. Rates use recorded actions/views, not unique visitors, verified subscribers or purchases. Do not directly compare them with unique-visitor capture rates.

Snapshots, selection and audience differences limit interpretation. Format/device comparisons are observational; the gamified subset is small. Embedded forms are excluded. Historical trigger, delay and frequency effects are not established. January–September does not predict Black Friday.

Practical hypotheses: clarify one primary action; test mobile usability, one format alternative or removing a nonessential field. Hold offer/targeting constant where possible. Track contact quality and commercial outcomes alongside submissions. These are experiments, not promised lifts. Use public aggregates only; never reproduce private customer identities, screenshots or campaign data.
