# Project brief and handoff worksheet

Use only the fields relevant to the requested task. Empty fields are questions or assumptions, never facts to invent.

## Inputs

- Store/category and verified URL:
- Requested outcome and scope (draft/audit/analysis/implementation):
- Commerce platform and email/capture tools:
- Audience/markets/language/time zone:
- Marketing eligibility and suppression source:
- Brand voice and approved product facts:
- Approved offer, commercial constraints and terms:
- Existing forms/flows/campaign cadence:
- Baseline period, traffic, audience counts and results:
- Available events/fields and data freshness:
- Permissions, owner and deadline:

## Output packet

1. Goal, verified facts, assumptions and blockers.
2. Priorities with expected impact, confidence, effort and dependencies.
3. Asset specs with complete copy and destinations.
4. Segment logic, exclusions and overlaps.
5. Flow entry/steps/exit/re-entry and failure paths, if applicable.
6. Offer/coupon and consent handling.
7. Measurement hypothesis, baseline, KPI and guardrails.
8. QA evidence, approvals needed and launch/rollback owner.

## Implementation record (only after authorized action)

- Account/store:
- Approved content/version and scope:
- Objects created/changed and IDs:
- Final state and verification time:
- Test evidence:
- Unresolved issues:
- Monitoring scope/stopping condition and owner:

## Example invocation prompts

“Draft a three-email welcome series for our homewares store. Keep our existing email provider. We offer no discounts. Include segmentation, purchase exit rules and full copy.”

“Audit this signup form and cart flow. Identify the top three fixes and what evidence is missing. Do not change the live account.”

“Compare a Poptin-led setup with retaining our existing stack for capture and welcome email. Separate verified product claims from account checks.”
