# Prompt guide — grok-imagine

Subject, then its action or effect, the feeling it carries, then a style label — one prompt string. Name the setting and light, or they are invented.

- **Register** — to generate, write comma-delimited noun fragments, ~10 words, not a sentence; to
  edit a supplied image, write a command — say what to change, then what to keep.
- **Realism** — ask in plain comparative terms for believable surfaces and fine detail — for example
  "natural skin and worn leather", "sharp fine-grain texture" — paired with what must stay unchanged.
- **Direction** — give the style or medium as a bare tag.
- **Tone** — attach the feeling to what expresses it, and reach for a precise mood word ("wistful",
  "brooding", "sunlit") over a flat one ("nice", "great").
