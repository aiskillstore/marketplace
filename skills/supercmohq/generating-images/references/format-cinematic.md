# Format — Cinematic still

For a single composed still at production standard, with control over shot, light and grade.

## Order of the prompt

- **Shot type** — how tight or wide the frame sits.
- **Subject** — who or what is in it; be specific.
- **Action** — what is happening.
- **Location** — where the scene sits.
- **Composition** — arrangement in frame, and where empty space falls.
- **Materials** — what things are made of; unsaid, surfaces go plastic.
- **Light** — direction, quality, time of day, and its effect.
- **Camera** — angle, lens, depth of field; aperture as a number.
- **Grade and medium** — the tone carried; one cast, one accent; grain if wanted.
- **Style and mood** — the aesthetic, and the feeling it lands.
- **Factual constraints** — accuracy requirements, stated outright.
- **Reference roles** — what each blended input is for.

## How to write it

Write sentences, not keywords — intent is read whole, so syntax tricks buy nothing. Reach for optical vocabulary over adjectives: shot type, angle, lens, aperture, depth of field, light direction, colour grade. Be direct and specific; the more you describe, the closer the result.
