# Black Gold

Financial-periodical order: a strong assertion, verifiable evidence, a precise reading of the evidence.
White pages hold charts and tables. Charcoal stages hold judgments and actions. Grayscale carries the
data; one gold focus closes the reading path. The cover rule is a structural accent, outside focal
counting. Exact tokens, frames and capacities live in `design.json`.

Prefer native lines for ordered observations, columns for period totals and horizontal bars for
rankings. Keep numeric labels on the marks; the caption names measure and unit. Different units use
aligned independent native plots when the runtime cannot completely project a combo for verification.
Never hide a missing series behind a successful conversion. Source, as-of and unit belong to every page.

The five-column matrix is a native table, with right-aligned numeric columns, horizontal rules and
share bars derived from the same numeric inputs. The bars are editable shapes; the values remain in
native cells. Cells use one authored paragraph and automatic wrapping; do not insert paragraph breaks
until whole-cell paragraph styling is verified for the active runtime.

Full-deck rhythm is cover, dark summary, white evidence, dark action close. Approximately one quarter
dark is a preference, not a reason to invent extra content. `meta.designScope: "excerpt"` explicitly
allows a long evidence excerpt. Short decks do not require absent narrative stages. Dark evidence
charts/grids are incompatible with this family and fail validation.

Keep titles left-aligned, clear and conclusive. Preserve breathing space between the title and dense
evidence. Density comes from data modules, not repeated cards. The three threshold bands are the
specific action-page form; they do not authorize card walls elsewhere.

Prose and reference-image conflicts are resolved explicitly. This implementation follows the prose's
dark-stage and single-focus identity; readable gold, source gray and watermark contrast are intentional
adaptations. It preserves the eight reference page roles, not exact raster positions or every small
secondary diagram. Gold pixel-area share is visually reviewed, not inferred from chart bounding boxes.

This skin does not impose investment advice, valuation models or allocation decisions on unrelated
content. Financial thesis, scenario, sensitivity and risk policy is separate; read the optional finance
policy only when the requested content requires it. All bundled numbers/claims are illustrative.
