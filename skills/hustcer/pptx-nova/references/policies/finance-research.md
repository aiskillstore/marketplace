# Optional finance research content policy

Use this reference when the user requests investment research, valuation, allocation or transaction
analysis. Selecting the Black Gold skin alone does not activate this policy or authorize financial
conclusions. This is an author/reviewer checklist, not an implemented financial validator.

- Distinguish verified observations, estimates and assumptions; retain source, measurement basis,
  unit and as-of date. Never treat the bundled illustrative dataset as market evidence.
- An investment conclusion needs its actual supporting evidence. A valuation conclusion needs an
  anchor such as comparables or a model cross-check and a sensitivity table. Return discussions need
  disclosed scenarios/assumptions; the renderer must not invent a point estimate or a probability.
- Pair risks with impact/probability where known, mitigation and owner. Do not fill unknown facts with
  plausible values. Preserve user content when selecting a visual format.
- A requested decision page identifies the decision, conditions, owner and next review/action time.
  The visual action page does not itself establish an investment recommendation.

The current Black Gold adapter checks finite data, source/as-of/unit presence, capacities, layout
roles and structural style. It does not verify financial arithmetic across prose, market facts,
valuation validity, legal disclosures or the quality of an investment assertion. Check those separately.
