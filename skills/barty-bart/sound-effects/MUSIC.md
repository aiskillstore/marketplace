# Suggested music

Free tracks that suit Claude motion graphics, grouped by feel. All are from Mixkit under the **Mixkit Stock Music Free License** (checked 13 Oct 2026). They are **not** stored in this repo: download the one you pick into the user's project at runtime from `https://assets.mixkit.co/music/<id>/<id>.mp3` and never commit it.

Before using one, do the licence check in `SKILL.md` (step 0): open the source page, confirm the licence hasn't changed, and confirm the user's use case is allowed.

Pass the track to the script like this, so `SOUND.md` and `CREDITS.txt` record it:
```
--music-source '"Tech House vibes" by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), Mixkit #130' --music-license "Mixkit Stock Music Free License"
```

## What the Mixkit Stock Music Free License allows (as of 13 Oct 2026)
- **Allowed:** YouTube videos, social media posts (Instagram, TikTok and others), online ads, podcasts, education, commercial and non-commercial. No credit required.
- **Not allowed:** CDs or DVDs, TV or radio broadcast, video games, remixing it or putting it in a music-only track, claiming it as your own, or registering it with Content ID or any rights management service.
- If a platform claims it, Mixkit asks you to forward the claim to team@mixkit.co.
- Licence page: https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree

## Other places to find music
Check each one's current terms against the user's use case before using it.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (YouTube Studio → Audio Library): free, for YouTube videos; some tracks need a credit line, shown next to the track.
- **Epidemic Sound**, **Artlist** and similar: paid subscriptions. The licence covers the channels and period of the user's subscription; ask the user if they have one.
- **The user's own track**: ask where it came from and what licence they have.
