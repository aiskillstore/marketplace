# Directing motion graphics

Read this when authored graphics, effects or a coordinated scene change the picture over time.
Motion graphics can explain a relationship, make a comparison felt, give a joke its timing or
establish a visual attitude. The work may use a small accent, a moving presenter and diagram,
or an entire animated passage. [Graphic composition](graphic-compositions.md) owns the hierarchy
of the picture they share; this page owns what their changes do to the viewing experience.

## Direct the change, not just the entrance

Understand what the viewer sees before an event, what changes, and what remains afterward. A board
can accumulate answers; a picture can be picked up, moved to a slot and rejected; a camera view can
recede while another idea takes the foreground. In each case the action gives the next state its
meaning. A satisfying entrance cannot compensate for a settled state that fails to show the claim.

Follow the identity of an object through its changes. When a card becomes an attachment, the same
card can keep its image, shape or trajectory; when a demonstrated interface changes style, its
recognizable controls can remain while its treatment changes. That continuity lets the audience
understand *what changed* without learning a new diagram at every beat. A clean cut is also a
strong handoff when the next idea is better understood immediately than through a journey.

An effect can serve feeling as well as literal explanation. A springy arrival may make an answer
playful; a sudden hard replacement may give a claim force; a slow, precise construction may make a
relationship easier to study. Choose the motion character that belongs to this film, then give the
result enough visible life for its content to register. The appropriate hold and pace depend on
the words, sound, picture size and amount the audience must read.

## Make the motion belong to one visual language

Palette, type, edges, texture, depth, motion and sound can reinforce one another. A retro cartoon
treatment, a precise mathematical diagram and a spatial 3D scene call for different choices about
weight, path, perspective and rhythm. Their names are creative directions, not renderer or component
types. Extract the actual relationships that make the chosen style convincing, and carry shared
decisions into the project's Recipes or ordinary component dependencies when several scenes use them.

Keep the scene's meaningful objects complete through a restyle. If a folder's contents, an editor's
controls or a comparison's result carry the idea, preserve their job as the look changes. Recoloring
only the outer panels changes less than the audience may think, while removing a label can require
the remaining layout to be recomposed. The film's Treatment owns its particular palette, ornament
and attitude.

Work with source media as part of that language. A moving viewport changes how time-bearing source footage is
seen; it does not change its performed time. An independently moving image, a drawn border
and a caption may share a moment without having the same visual owner. When footage and graphics
must move as one scene, their shared geometry can be directed together. [Visual Clip](../../production/visual-clips.md)
owns time-bearing footage, and [component design](../../production/component-design.md)
owns the implementation boundary.

## Give events and movement different jobs

An explanation, reveal or comparison can respond to a Script Selection or Moment; a silent animated
passage can use authored time. The reference locates the behavior and its meaningful changes in
the work; travel, settling, texture and secondary motion can be designed locally around them.
[Timing](../../production/timing.md) and [component design](../../production/component-design.md#let-meaning-drive-the-behavior)
own the exact projection and author inputs.

Derive related movement from the relationships it depicts. A pointer arrives at the control it
presses; a badge follows the object it labels; a rejected drag returns from the place it reached.
That keeps a layout revision from breaking the visual action. Coordinate entrance, response,
persistence and exit across a scene, while leaving independent Caption, sound or media contributions
free to serve their own roles.

Inspect the changing passage with its actual material and sound. Exact frames can show whether a
shape, overlap or settled state reads; playback reveals weight, pace and whether the handoff lands.
Judge the effect at delivery size and in the neighboring picture, including the moment after its
most impressive motion. [Snapshots](../../production/snapshots.md) and
[review](../../production/review.md) provide those views; [drawing](../../production/component-visuals.md)
owns how the selected visual behavior is rendered.
