# Image direction examples

Read these with [Directing generated images](../image-direction.md). Each complete prompt uses
Capture, Person, Shot and Setting. The Kit supplies Capture and assembles `person`, `shot` and
`setting` in that order; the [Craft](../image-direction.md#use-a-concrete-capture-direction) explains
assembly. For these full-screen portraits, set `aspect-ratio="9:16"` and usually `resolution="2K"`
on `gpt:Image`.

These production prompts belong to the accepted balcony portrait and five reviewed character
portraits. They retain the same capture language while choosing the person, framing and setting
for each picture: soft goth, clean girl, coquette, Harajuku decora and streetwear. The styling terms
organize a particular appearance; the selected details make that direction specific.

## Japanese sailor on an apartment balcony

![Sailor-style presenter on a balcony, with navy clothing, terracotta tiles and green plants](https://storage.googleapis.com/hypit-public-assets/assets/skills/hypit/image-direction/2026-09-10/japanese-sailor-balcony.webp)

The user selected the result generated from this exact prompt. The Person paragraph gives a
specific casting direction, strong beauty, broad shoulders and head-to-shoulder proportions, with
long straight hair and sailor styling. Shot establishes the frontal speaking state, free-hand
gestures and face placement. The table gives the offset composition a physical relationship.

In the selected result, navy clothing, terracotta architecture and plants have distinct color roles.
The glass door, railing and neighboring buildings establish a place with different distances.
The model supplied individual tiles, leaves, reflections and clothing details within the authored
direction; those incidental arrangements are not additional requirements for another portrait.

Production prompt:

```text
A photograph captured as a single frame from a video actually shot on an iPhone, with the texture of real iPhone footage. The image looks real, without an oily, overprocessed finish. The background is clearly visible, with no depth-of-field blur. Skin texture is natural and fine, and the lighting is natural. The image is coherent and free of visual artifacts.

A Japanese woman in her early twenties. She is exceptionally, strikingly beautiful, with the looks of a top Korean girl-group idol and very fair skin. She has beautiful long, straight black hair with blunt bangs and pretty makeup, with bold eye makeup giving her an intensely alluring gaze. She has a beautiful figure, very broad shoulders and excellent head-to-shoulder proportions. She wears a sweet-but-edgy navy-blue sailor-style outfit with white trim and a matching sailor cap.

Generate a vertical medium half-body shot. She sits on a chair, fairly close to the camera, holding a handheld microphone and speaking directly to the viewer, with her free hand gesturing. Her face points straight toward the lens, with no head tilt or rotation, and sits toward the upper-right part of the frame. A small wooden table beside her enters the lower-left corner.

She is on an apartment balcony. Behind her are a sliding glass door, textured exterior tiles, metal railings and green plants, with neighboring buildings visible beyond the balcony. The background color scheme is terracotta and green.
```

The frontal face and speaking gesture give a reusable direct-to-viewer state. The upper-right face
and lower-left table are choices for this composition. A centered standing view keeps the casting
and proportion direction while changing posture, placement and the scene relationship. A podcast
view instead directs attention toward the partner. See the [idle guidance](../image-direction.md#choose-an-idle-state-for-the-encounter).

## Streetwear guy beside a basketball court

![Streetwear presenter in a navy graphic shirt beside a terracotta-and-teal basketball court](https://storage.googleapis.com/hypit-public-assets/assets/skills/hypit/image-direction/2026-09-10/streetwear-basketball.webp)

A hip-hop-star beauty comparison, short twists and relaxed streetwear establish the person. The bench and open court explain the offset framing, with terracotta and teal giving the surroundings a deliberate palette.

```text
A photograph captured as a single frame from a video actually shot on an iPhone, with the texture of real iPhone footage. The image looks real, without an oily, overprocessed finish. The background is clearly visible, with no depth-of-field blur. Skin texture is natural and fine, and the lighting is natural. The image is coherent and free of visual artifacts.

An African-American man in his early twenties. He is exceptionally, strikingly handsome, with the looks of a young hip-hop star, brown skin and a magnetic, confident presence. He has short twists beneath a backward baseball cap and expressive eyes. He has an athletic build, very broad shoulders and excellent head-to-shoulder proportions. He wears relaxed New York streetwear: an oversized navy-blue graphic T-shirt and a silver chain.

Generate a vertical medium half-body shot. He sits on a courtside bench, fairly close to the camera, holding a handheld microphone and speaking directly to the viewer, with his free hand gesturing. His face points straight toward the lens, with no head tilt or rotation, and sits toward the upper-left part of the frame. The bench extends beside him toward the open court on the right.

He is beside an outdoor neighborhood basketball court in New York. Behind him are the painted court, a chain-link fence, a mural and trees beyond the fence. The background color scheme is terracotta and teal.
```
